nl_blocks IS a full second copy of the corpus and it is NOT being fixed. It
fits with a factor of 7 to spare, and making it lazy would put a third
unmeasured change inside the artifact that the identity gate is meant to hold
still. It is listed rather than hidden: at rung-2 scale it is the next thing
that breaks, and it should be found here rather than at 2000 seconds.

READ THE INSTRUMENT'S HISTORY BEFORE TRUSTING THIS NUMBER. The first version of
peak_gib() printed "0.0 GiB" for every build in the identity gate: it called
GetProcessMemoryInfo without argtypes, the -1 pseudo-handle from
GetCurrentProcess truncated on a 64-bit HANDLE, the call failed, and the zeroed
struct was reported as a measurement. No exception was raised. It was fixed and
then verified on a known positive BEFORE this build ran:

    baseline peak 0.029 GiB -> 3.029 GiB after a touched 3 GiB allocation,
    delta 3.000 GiB.  CONTROL FIRES.

11.2 GiB is a reading from an instrument that has been shown to move. The 0.0
figures in code_data_identity_gate.txt are from the same instrument before it
was shown to move, and are void; that log carries the correction inline.
