Prospective direction after audited PQT-009-R1, 6 October 2026. No executable
protocol, new representation or output access yet; this is the next research
question, not authorization to improvise selections or declare promotion.

even with broader calibration. Coverage helps some held-out metrics, but does
not alone establish a practical method. Separate one structural intervention
from an arbitrary increase in fitting cost: does restoring only the original
shared embedding/readout materially change the behavior of the frozen broad

ternary baselines, original F32 shared-parameter restoration as an excess-storage

cannot qualify as pure ternary or satisfy the existing storage gate. A low-bit

identities and original tie must be independently checked. GPU reconstruction
does not establish native speed or memory savings.

Use the already consumed R1 Wiki/news token identities only for this structural

representation lower bound. This also does not repair the original standalone
