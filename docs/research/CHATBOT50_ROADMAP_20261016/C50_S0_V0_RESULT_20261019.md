## What the numbers say, and what they do not

- **The engine runs at its desk model, not below it.** Every weight organ streams at 31-37 GB/s, so the DESK-MODEL
  (32 GB/s plus 2 ms) fits within 6.1% at all three contexts. The registered predictions assumed a 10-30% loss to
  the desk that is not there.
- **Context costs through the KV read alone.** From 512 to 4096 the KV organ grows from 1.6 to 10.2 ms while every
  other organ stays flat. The KV read runs at about 16 GB/s, half the weights' rate: it is the one organ below the
  stream rate.
- **Phase 2's work order** (ROADMAP §7) comes from this table: at 2048 the routed experts and the head together are
  55% of the step, and at 4096 the KV read is 30%.
- **Scope:** one box (this 3600X), one artifact (T0), decode only. The organ times are wall laps of the `--organs`
  variant. These are rates on an empty machine, by the protocol; other machines are Phase 3.
