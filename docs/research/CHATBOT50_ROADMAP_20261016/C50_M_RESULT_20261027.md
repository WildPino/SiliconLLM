## What it says

- **No reading flips, and no re-applied rule changes outcome.** The verdicts of S4 (FAIL), T1 part 2b (v0 above T0)
  and N (FAIL) stand. By the protocol's rule, "the limit is the teacher data" and "N closed" are not marked to be
  reread.
  - Under R_acc, E2 − v0 is still positive (+0.017).
  - N is now significantly below E2 (−0.014).
- **The sizes were overstated.** v0 recovers 0.673 of the gap on R, at most 0.500 once temperature is removed, and
  0.231 on top-1 accuracy. **Most of the "recovered gap" reported since S4 was temperature.**
- **Arm a** looked like half the gap (R 0.505). Calibrated it is 0.18, and on accuracy it is below T0.
- **R_acc has its own noise.** Its null (N-shuffle against N) moves by ±0.015, and the CIs are 0.02-0.12 wide on these
  36 FIT documents. Differences of 0.01 between arms cannot be read on it. Part 2 (72 post-release documents) is where
  it can become the primary.
