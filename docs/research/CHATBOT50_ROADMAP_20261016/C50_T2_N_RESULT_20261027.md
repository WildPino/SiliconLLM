## What it says

- **The experts learn the same signal a LoRA learns on a second pass, and no more.** N stores 239 M new parameters
  (+1.56% of v0's routed experts) and halves E2's validation KL drop. On the documents it ties E2 within the noise
  (−0.0013, CI touching 0), and on the items neither moves.
- **About half of N's gain does not depend on which expert holds what.** The shuffle keeps +0.0058 of +0.0103 (56%).
  Part of what the branch adds is a generic correction, which any trained layer would make.
- **The gate fires on 98% of (token, layer) pairs** (docs 0.980; panels 0.983-0.986; layer 19 lowest, 0.918). No
  fire cost was trained, and the branch became a dense addition. At that rate its desk price is 0.98 × 36.1 + 0.31 ≈
  35.7 MB per token, +4.1% of v0's 867.3 MB, for no gain over E2, which costs no bytes.
- **The binding limit is the teacher data, not the capacity** (the registered reading of a FAIL with E2 near v0).
  The 9.5 M positions of block 1 have been taken twice now (A1, then E2 or N). A second pass of either kind buys about
  0.01 of R.

## What this cannot say

- **One seed, one K (4), one size (+1.56%), one recipe.** A FAIL closes N at this size and volume, not distillation
  into experts in general.
- **The same 9.5 M teacher positions v0 learned from.** Through E2, the cell says that a second pass over them buys
  about 0.01 of R, whatever is trained. It does not say what new teacher data would buy.
- **Quality only.** The bytes are a desk price; no engine runs the branch.

## Against the thesis

The thesis wants F0's knowledge in auxiliary experts on v0. The two routes tried now both stop at the same wall:
- **X (F0's own experts):** no stitch carries v0's state into them. Frozen.
- **N (experts distilled from F0):** they learn only what block 1 still holds after v0, about 0.01 of R, the same as
  a LoRA's second pass.

Distillation into any organ is bounded by the teacher positions. The next lever for the thesis is more F0 teacher
data (S3 blocks 2 onward, about 32 h of local GPU per 10 M positions), or a new route into F0's own weights. Either
is a decision to make.
