  the `quantize.imatrix.file` string: backslashes, where A2's command had used forward slashes. The wrapper now
  passes every path with forward slashes, and the rerun matched P0 exactly.

## What this says, and what it does not

- **It says** that S4's LoRA gain is not an artifact of the PyTorch instrument or of the unmerged adapter. As a
  plain Q4_K_M file that the engine can load, T1-c48 recovers 0.70 of the T0 → F0 document gap. That is 0.170 BPB
  out of 0.244, at T0's size.
- **It does not say** that T1 is a better chatbot overall: it loses closed-book knowledge on nq and tqa, and
  36 documents and 206 items are proxies. One LoRA, one seed, one merge route.
