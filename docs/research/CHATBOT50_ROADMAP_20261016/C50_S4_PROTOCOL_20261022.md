# C50-S4 protocol: does added capacity beat a matched LoRA control at m ≈ 2?

22 October 2026. Cell S4 of the [CHATBOT50 roadmap](ROADMAP.md) §5, Phase 1. Design and feasibility checks:
[C50_S4_DESIGN_20261022](C50_S4_DESIGN_20261022.md) (MVE-1 to MVE-4). Pushed before any arm is trained, before the
evaluator exists and before any S4 score is read.

## Question

On T0's frozen weights, with the same teacher data, recipe and order for every arm, does trained capacity, either
(a) extra routed experts or (b) a conditional memory bank, recover more of the T0 → F0 gap than a LoRA control with
the same trainable count? If neither does beyond the margin below, capacity transfer is closed **at this budget**
(ROADMAP §5 S4), not in general.

## Arms (as in the design note, with its amendment and MVE-3)

| Arm | What is trained | Trainable | Its control |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| **a** | 5,320 new expert slots (S1's m2 layout): frozen copies plus rank-4 deltas, new router rows; the copies' selection bias starts below every original's and rises by the load rule below | 174,325,760 | c496 |
| **b** | a frozen bank of 1.49 M trigram/bigram rows, read after the attention of layers 4 and 12 by a trained key map, value map and gate | 16,781,312 | c48 |
| **c496** | LoRA rank 496 on the always-on modules of all 20 layers | 174,465,024 | |
| **c48** | LoRA rank 48 on the same modules | 16,883,712 | |

- **T0 itself** is evaluated in the same instrument and is the baseline of every comparison.
- **Every arm starts as T0.** Before its first step, each run compares the arm with T0 on 512 tokens on the GPU and
  does not train unless the max abs logit difference is 0. MVE-3 found arm (a) off by 2.53 on the GPU; the fix is in
  `c50_s4_student.py` (8de2c18), exact on the CPU, and this check is its GPU test.
- **Arm (a)'s load rule (fixed now).** After each step, every copy's selection bias moves by
  0.01 × sign(mean load − its load); the originals' biases stay T0's. MVE-3 used 0.005 and no copy entered in 27
  steps; at 0.01 the offsets (1.12-1.51) are consumed in at most ~150 of the ~1,280 steps. The share of picks taken by
  the copies is recorded at every step and reported as a curve.

- **General panel:** S2's items outside the donor-gap panel (nq 502, tqa 483, mmlu 491, arc 495, gsm8k 90) and the

- **Rules, as in S2 and A4:** the same inputs and ID files; documents scored over their full span in one context;

## Training recipe (the same for every arm)

- **Data:** block 1's shards 0-469 (22,560 documents, 9,468,705 scored positions), one epoch, in stored order,
  documents packed to 8,192 tokens per step (~1,280 steps). Shards 470-493 are the validation slice.
- **Loss:** the 33-bucket KL from F0's top-32 to the student, summed over positions and divided by their count.
- **Optimizer:** AdamW (0.9, 0.999), weight decay 0, peak lr 1e-4, 30 steps of linear warm-up, then cosine to 1e-5
  at the last step; global gradient-norm clip 1.0 after unscaling; fp16 forward with fp32 trainables and gradient
  scaling, as in MVE-3. Seed 20261016. One run per arm.
- **Validation:** the KL on shards 470-471 at step 0, every 250 steps and at the end. Curves only, never a gate,
  except the training guard below.
- **Arm (b)'s bank** (`c50_s4_bank.py`, built on the CPU before the run):
  - an occurrence is an n-gram ending at token t of a training document with t below the document's last token;
    a trigram row exists if it occurs at least twice, else a bigram row if it does (MVE-4: 1,492,153 rows; the
    count is re-taken under this definition and reported);
  - value = the mean over its occurrences of Σ_k p_k W_out[k], with F0's top-32 probabilities at position t + 1
    and T0's output embedding (dequantized fp32), stored in fp16 with its count;
  - **leave-one-out during training:** at a training position the read value excludes that position's own
    contribution, (n · v − own) / (n − 1), so the bank cannot hand the student the teacher's answer at that very
    position. Evaluation reads the full mean.
- **Sessions:** Kaggle T4 x2, at most 9.5 h of training per session, checkpoints (trainables, optimizer, scaler,
  data position, arm (a)'s biases) every 100 steps and at the cap, resumed from the previous session's output.
  Arms run on the three accounts in parallel; one session per account at a time.

## Gates (fixed now; `c50_s4_verdict.py`)

**Recovered share.** On the 36 S4 documents, pooled:
R(X) = (bits_T0 − bits_X) / (bits_T0,S2 − bits_F0,S2).
The numerator comes from the PyTorch evaluator (T0 and X in the same instrument); the denominator is S2's llama.cpp
gap on the same documents. For each capacity arm X with its control C: **Δ_X = R(X) − R(C)**. Its CI95 comes from
10,000 paired document-bootstrap draws (seed 20261016) that recompute numerator and denominator.

| Gate | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary** | Δ_X ≥ 0.20, and the CI95 of Δ_X lies above 0 |
| **Secondary 1: S4 donor-gap items** | X not worse than C, pooled over the 206 items: with b = items C gets right and X wrong, c = the reverse, b − c ≤ max(2√(b + c), 1) (A4's paired rule) |
| **Secondary 2: general panel** | no regression against T0 in the PyTorch instrument, on each of nq, tqa, mmlu, arc, gsm8k and chat, by the same paired rule |
| **Controls, on the same checkpoint** | each control k gives Δ_k = R(X_k) − R(C). Remove and shuffle erase the gain iff Δ_k ≤ Δ_X / 2. Mask, which hides a share s of the S4 documents' lookups, passes iff Δ_mask ≤ Δ_X × (1 − s / 2): at least half of the drop that content-driven gain would show. All controls of the arm must pass |

- **Arm (b)'s controls:** remove (no row read anywhere); shuffle (values permuted among rows, within bins of 1,000
  rows of adjacent count, seed 20261016); mask (rows ranked by count, every other row masked; the masked half's share
  of the S4 documents' lookups is reported).
- **Arm (a)'s controls:** remove (no copy can be picked; this is T0 exactly, so R = 0 by construction and it is
  checked by the identity test, not scored); shuffle (each copy's deltas and router row exchanged with another copy's
  of the same group and layer, seed 20261016).
- **Training guard:** an arm whose final validation KL is not below T0's on the same slice did not train. Its
  comparisons are INCONCLUSIVE, not FAIL, and the run is reviewed (rule 3).

**Outcome per capacity arm:** PASS iff primary, both secondaries and all its controls pass, written **"quality PASS,
invariance pending"** until S1's engine check runs on the trained artifact (the winner only). **NOT PASSED** if the
primary passes and anything else fails: rule 3's design review. **FAIL** if the primary fails.

**Outcome of S4:** PASS if arm (a) or arm (b) passes; FAIL (capacity transfer closed at this budget) if both fail
the primary; otherwise NOT PASSED, with the design review.

**The verdict script's planted cases (`--selftest`), run before the first evaluation:** a capacity arm with a gain
its control lacks and that its controls erase (PASS); the same gain also in the control (FAIL); a gain that survives
the shuffle (NOT PASSED); a general-panel regression (NOT PASSED); an untrained arm (INCONCLUSIVE).

## Evaluator fidelity, before any arm is scored (MVE-5)

The PyTorch evaluator must reproduce S2's T0 (A4's P0 outputs, llama.cpp) closely enough for S2's gap to be its
denominator.
- **Documents:** on the 36 S4 documents, |bits_T0,PyTorch − bits_T0,S2| / bytes ≤ 0.010 BPB pooled (MVE-2: mean
  dlogp −0.0019 nats per token against P0).
- **General panel:** on each item panel, PyTorch T0 against llama.cpp T0 by A4's paired rule.
- **Also reported:** the S4 donor-gap items PyTorch T0 gets right. llama.cpp T0 gets all of them wrong by
  construction, so some regression to the mean is expected; it is common to every arm.
- **A failure stops the line** for a design review before any arm is scored.

## Order, budget and stop rules

1. **Build:** `c50_s4_bank.py` (CPU, local, ~1-2 h), `c50_s4_train.py` (training and resume),
   `c50_s4_eval.py` (evaluator), `c50_s4_verdict.py` (with `--selftest`), Kaggle tasks; a CPU smoke of each. Arm
   (b)'s bank goes up as a dataset; T0, block 1 and the code go to the two other accounts.
2. **MVE-5** (evaluator fidelity, T0 only): ~1-2 h of T4 x2.
3. **Trainings:** a, b, c48, c496 on the three accounts, each with its GPU identity check first.
4. **Evaluations:** T0 is MVE-5's. Then c48, c496, b, a, b's three controls and a's shuffle: 8 evaluations of
   ~1-1.5 h each (ESTIMATE from MVE-2: a forward of 512 tokens ~5.3 s, a MoE layer's dequantization ~0.15 s).
5. **Verdict,** from the records only.

**T4 work, announced (rule 7).**
- **Reason:** S4 (ROADMAP §5).
- **Budget:** ≤ 75 session-hours on T4 x2 in total. From MVE-3, per pass over the training split: b 8.8 h, c48
  10.1 h, c496 10.9 h, a 10.0-18.9 h (rising with the copies' share), about 40-49 h; evaluations ~10 h; MVE-5 ~2 h.
  It stays inside the granted 90 h per week, at most 30 per account.
- **Stop rules:**
  - a failed identity check or a failed MVE-5 stops the line for a design review (rule 3), not a retry;
  - an OOM, a non-finite loss or a crash is recorded as VOID, kept, and reviewed before any rerun;
  - the 75 h budget is not exceeded without a new announcement.

## What this cannot say

- **One seed and one recipe per arm.** No rate is tuned for any arm; the result holds for this recipe, and the
  validation curves are reported so that an undertrained arm is visible.
- **The budget is small:** ~9.5 M teacher positions. A FAIL closes capacity transfer at this volume.
- **Arm (a)'s learned capacity is the deltas (163 M), not the copies**; its stored size is S1's m2. **Arm (b) stands
  at m ≈ 1.62** of T0's stored bytes (MVE-4), below 2.
- **36 documents and 206 items** judge S4; the panels are proxies (S2).
- **Quality only.** Speed invariance on the trained artifact is S1's check, still pending at a PASS.
