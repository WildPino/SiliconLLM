# C50 cell M protocol: how much of R is temperature? (a metric test, no training)

27 October 2026. Ordered by the [review directive](C50_DIRETTIVA_REVISIONE_20261027.md) (section 3, point 4).
Registered before any of its evaluations runs, except the smoke runs below.

## Question

R, the share of S2's T0 → F0 bits gap recovered on S4's 36 documents, judged S4, C50-T1 part 2b and option N. The
audit (`results/c50_audit_r_hedging.json`) found that most of R sits where the arm is wrong anyway: on top-1 accuracy
v0 recovers 0.231 of the gap, not 0.673. **How much of R does one temperature buy, and do the readings that rested on
R still hold?**

## What it measures

**`c50_m_eval.py`**, one pass per arm on T4 × 2, nothing trained:
- **Temperature fits** on the validation shards 470-471 (49 k positions, outside the documents). The grid runs from
  0.50 to 2.50 in steps of 0.025, and the same two rules apply to every arm:
  - **τ_nll** minimises the NLL of the corpus targets (Guo et al.'s rule);
  - **τ_kl** minimises the 33-bucket KL to F0 (`kl33`, the training objective).
- **On the documents:**
  - each target's log-probability at every τ;
  - the target's rank at τ = 1;
  - `docs.pos.bin` at τ = 1, which must match the arm's recorded evaluation.

**`c50_m_verdict.py`** (self-test 9/9), from the records only:
- **R** at τ = 1, as recorded.
- **The trivial controls,** T0 alone:
  - R(T0 at τ_nll) and R(T0 at τ_kl), both fit off the documents;
  - R(T0 at its oracle τ on the 36 documents), the strongest any single temperature can be.
  - Each is reported as a share of R(v0) and of R(a).
- **R_cal,** each arm at its τ_nll against T0 at its own, over the gap left after T0's temperature. F0 stays at τ = 1,
  and F0 is overconfident (top-1 confidence 0.8165 against accuracy 0.744), so **R_cal is an upper bound.**
- **R_acc** on top-1 accuracy, which no temperature moves:
  - with and without the decile of documents (4 of 36) where F0 gains most over T0, a set defined on F0 − T0 only;
  - beside it, top-5 accuracy and the target's median rank, for partial knowledge.
- **The readings,** as differences with a paired document bootstrap (10,000 draws) under R, R_cal and R_acc:

| Reading | Rested on |
| --- | --- |
| S4: a − c496, b − c48 | S4 FAIL |
| T1 part 2b: v0 − T0 | v0 = T0 + A1 |
| N − v0, N − E2, N-shuffle − v0 | N FAIL |
| E2 − v0 | "the limit is the teacher data" |
| N-shuffle − N | R_acc's own null (a shuffle should not move accuracy) |

- **The original rules re-applied:** S4's primary (Δ ≥ 0.20 with CI95 > 0) and N's (≥ 0.05 over v0 and over E2),
  each margin rescaled by metric(v0) / R(v0).
  - T1 part 2b's G1 is an absolute threshold on R (0.559). Rescaled by v0 it is a tautology, so it is not re-applied.

**Arms:** T0, c48, c496, a, b (S4, on T0's GGUF) and v0, E2, N, N-shuffle (on v0's GGUF), each with its recorded
training's weights.

## Rule

| Check | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| STOP | any of: an arm's τ = 1 log-probabilities differ from its recorded evaluation by more than 1e-3 (the observed maximum is recorded); a τ at the grid's edge; a wrong GGUF (T0 `44de7315…`, v0 `e490f803…`), mode or bank; an incomplete run |
| **R DEMOTED** | a fitted control (τ_nll or τ_kl) takes ≥ 0.25 of R(v0). R decides nothing alone: R_cal and R_acc stand beside it |
| **R KEPT WITH R_acc** | even the oracle temperature takes < 0.25 of R(v0). R stays, always with R_acc beside it |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | otherwise. R stays suspect |
| A reading **flips** | the new metric's CI95 excludes 0 on the other side |
| A reading **loses CI** | reported as underpowered, not as a contradiction |

**Outputs:**
- a short result and a CORRECTION row in §12;
- every reading that flips, or whose re-applied rule changes outcome, is marked to be reread, including "the limit is
  the teacher data" and "N closed";
- X's closure rested on routing overlap and cosine, not R: reported as not affected.

**R_acc becomes the primary only after part 2,** on the 72 post-release documents. These 36 are FIT documents: on
three of them (58, 62, 40) F0's accuracy jumps by 0.18-0.32, which is about 36% of the accuracy gap.

**Why 0.25:** a quarter of v0's gain from one scalar on the logits is a part no organ should be credited with.

## Literature and its known failure modes (rule 2)

- **Temperature scaling:** Guo et al. 2017, *On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks*
  ([1706.04599](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.04599v2)), read in the paper.
  - "T is optimized with respect to NLL on the validation set."
  - "Temperature scaling does not affect the model's accuracy."
  - It assumes the validation and test sets "are drawn from the same distribution".
- **Its known failure, dataset shift:** Ovadia et al. 2019 ([1906.02530](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02530)), abstract
  only: "traditional post-hoc calibration does indeed fall short".
  - Here the fit set (wiki, edu, math) is not the documents' mix, which includes 12 code documents.
  - That is why the oracle control exists: a mis-fit τ weakens the fitted controls. It can only push toward INCONCLUSIVE,
    never toward KEEP.

## The thesis (rule 4)

**No outcome moves the thesis.** This is a metric test, with a metric test's budget. It decides which number judges the
next cell (the injection test of directive point 5) before that cell spends anything.

## Independent review (rule 6)

A fresh sub-agent, with no access to the conversation, was given the thesis (§0, §5), the directive, this protocol and
the code. Its verdict was "register after fixes". All six objections were taken:

| # | Objection | Severity | Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A τ fit by KL to F0 on the trained arms' own objective and distribution gives τ ≈ 1 by construction, so R_cal would be R rescaled | blocking | τ_nll (Guo's rule) is the primary fit; τ_kl is reported; an oracle τ on the documents is added |
| 2 | A low trivial share cannot vindicate R, and an off-domain τ pushes toward KEEP | blocking | three outcomes: KEEP needs even the oracle below 0.25; shares are also given against R(a) |
| 3 | F0 gets no temperature, which inflates R_cal; the gap mixes instruments | should-fix | R_cal is labelled an upper bound; F0's τ comes in part 2; the mix stays within MVE-5's 0.010 BPB |
| 4 | R_acc has no null, is dominated by memorised documents, and near-tie noise drives it | should-fix | R_acc becomes primary only after part 2; it is also reported without the F0-gain decile; top-5 and median rank are added; N-shuffle − N is its null |
| 5 | The change rule flags noise and misses the 0.673 → 0.231 drop | should-fix | "flips" needs the new CI to exclude 0 on the other side; "loses CI" is reported as underpowered; S4's and N's rules are re-applied with rescaled margins |
| 6 | The checks skip a_mode, bank_mode and T0's absolute sha; fp16 reproducibility | note | all three are checked; the observed maximum Δ logp is recorded; the smokes were bit for bit |
