# C50-S0 protocol: T0's measured speed v0, with an organ breakdown

17 October 2026. Cell S0 of the [CHATBOT50 roadmap](ROADMAP.md) §5, Phase 1. Pushed before any S0
process runs, and before A1b runs.

## Question

- **What S0 measures:** the engine's own decode rate on T0 (P0, `44de7315…`) at contexts 512,
  2048 and 4096, with its confidence interval and dispersion. It also measures where the time per
  token goes, organ by organ.
- **Why:** v0 is the base of every Phase-1 ratio. S1 tests v(m) / v_pred(m) against it, and
  Phase 2 reads its work order from the breakdown.
- **Not a target.** v0 is not gated. Desk rates are labelled DESK-MODEL; a rate is quoted only
  from this run, with its CI.

## Preconditions (the S0 qualification of ROADMAP §5)

- **Already PASS:** A3 (engine fidelity on T0) and A8 (chat in C).
- **The A4 verdict comes first.** The rate session runs only after `c50_a4_verdict.py` has
  passed T0.
  - If A4 fails, T0 becomes the cheapest higher-precision exact-head artifact that passes
    (Q5_K_M, then Q6_K; ROADMAP §5).
  - The model pin in `c50_s0_run.py` is then replaced by an addendum pushed before the session.
- **The A6 verdict** (the FIT ranking of the exact-head policies) is recorded for Phase 2. S0 does
  not wait for it.
- **Stability:** A1b runs first in the same session, unchanged
  ([protocol](C50_A1B_TIMING_STABILITY_PROTOCOL_20261017.md); see its addendum 1 below).

## A1b addendum 1 (tooling only, before any A1b process)

- **The problem:** S0 extends `c50_engine.c`. The A1b runner hashed that file next to the
  pinned A1b binary, so the recorded source hash would no longer have matched the binary.
- **The fix:** the A1b source is frozen as `c50_engine_a1b.c`. It is a byte copy of the 7ab2048
  `c50_engine.c` (sha256 `f874031b…`).
- **The runner now:** records the hash of that copy, and refuses to run unless
  `bin/c50_engine_a1b.exe` is the pinned binary `6cbafb90…`.
- **Unchanged:** nothing else in A1b, including its arms, order, gate and verdict script.

## Engine changes for S0

All are built from this commit's `c50_engine.c` (sha256 `47736ad3…`). The A1b and A3 binaries are
not rebuilt.

| Change | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `--organs` (default off, `organs=0/1` on the CONFIG line) | Times each organ per token by laps of one clock on the main thread: attention (norms, QKV, Q/K norm and RoPE, output projection), kv (cache write, attention over the cache, combine), dense (layer 0's FFN), router (router matvec and selection), routed, shared, head (final norm and output matrix), and other (embedding, FFN norms, activation quantization, residual sums). With `--organs`, the routed and shared experts run as separate jobs, so they can be timed apart. |
| Byte count | The bench line reports, for every process, the mean bytes each organ streams per timed token. These come from the tensors' row sizes, and for kv from the positions read. |
| RAM | The bench line reports the process's peak working set and peak commit. |
| Planted binary `c50_engine_s0_plant_head.exe` | Built with `-DC50_PLANT_HEAD_TWICE`: the output matrix is computed twice per token, and the CONFIG line prints `plant=head_twice`. |

- **Binaries:**
  - `bin/c50_engine_s0.exe`, sha256 `786e1c6d…`;
  - `bin/c50_engine_s0_plant_head.exe`, sha256 `f2cd3401…`;

## Instrument and schedule

- **Mode:** `--bench`, 128 timed steps after a fill of `ctx` tokens, on fresh processes.
  - The fill is sequential decode: the engine has no batched prefill. The time to the first
    token of a ctx-token prompt is therefore the fill time, and it is reported as such.
  - The memory mode and priority are the A1b adopted arm's. If A1b fails, the run uses the
    complete arm with the smallest max/min, and v0 is labelled UNSTABLE. If no A1b arm is
    complete, S0 is VOID.
- **Documents:** these are timing inputs from the A4 FIT document panel, and no quality is read.
  - At 512 and 2048, document 0 (2,300 tokens).
  - At 4096, document 1 (4,617 tokens), the only panel document with ≥ 4,224 tokens.
- **Order** (`c50_s0_run.py`, fixed now):
  1. Model check: the runner hashes the GGUF and requires `44de7315…`.
  2. Pre-flight: 45 s, admissible iff the mean foreign occupancy is below 4.39% (the E52 bar,
     under A1 addendum 2).
  3. Planted control: 3 pairs at context 512 in fresh processes, each pair `--organs` then
     planted `--organs`.
  4. Ten rounds. Round r visits the contexts rotated by r (512, 2048, 4096; then 2048, 4096,
     512; and so on), so that every context sees every position in the round.
     - Each visit is one `--organs`-off process.
     - In rounds 0-4 at 2048, and rounds 0-2 at 512 and 4096, the visit is followed by one
       `--organs` process at the same context.
- **Occupancy:** each process is split into its own and foreign occupancy (E44).
  - A process at or above the bar is VOID and replaced.
  - More than 5 replacements in one arm make the run VOID. An arm is the binary, the context
    and the organs flag.
  - A failed process or a CONFIG line that does not confirm the arm makes the run VOID.
- **Scripts:** the runner `c50_s0_run.py` prints numbers only, and `c50_s0_report.py` computes
  everything below.
  - The report chooses nothing. The arm comes from the A1b verdict file, by the rule above.
  - A logic check with shrunken constants (contexts 64-256, 4 steps, 2 rounds, the bar disabled,
    on the busy box) ran the whole path, including the UNSTABLE adoption from a synthetic A1b FAIL.
    None of its numbers is a rate.

## What is reported (no gate on v0)

**v0 per context,** from the 10 admissible `--organs`-off processes (one median tok/s each):
- the mean of the medians, its CI95 (mean ± t(0.975, 9) · sd / √10) and the lower bound;
- min..max, max/min and CV;
- the label (STABLE iff A1b passed). S0's own max/min at 2048 is printed next to the A1b bar of
  1.06, not gated;
- the engine's bytes per token, the DESK-MODEL rate (bytes / 32 GB/s + 2 ms) and the measured /
  desk ratio. Also the effective GB/s (bytes × v0);
- the sequential fill rate and the time to first token for a ctx-token prompt;
- RAM: the largest peak working set and peak commit. With `--mem private` the peak working set
  also counts the file mapping during the copy, so the resident footprint at steady state is the
  commit;
- the within-process max/min step, the foreign occupancy and the replacements.

**Organ breakdown,** from the `--organs` processes:
- per organ: the mean ms, its share of the step, MB/token and effective GB/s;
- the instrumentation overhead: the mean step with organs over the mean step without, same
  context, same rounds;
- the lap closure: the sum of the organs over the step.

**The breakdown's own gate (planted control).**
- **Rule:** over the 3 pairs, the median head-time ratio planted / base must be ≥ 1.7, and the
  median ratio of all other organs' time must lie inside [0.90, 1.12].
- **On FAIL:** the breakdown is reported as UNVALIDATED, and Phase 2 does not take its work
  order from it. v0 is unaffected, because v0 comes from `--organs`-off processes.
- **Why the rule needs both halves:** a clock that charged the extra head pass to every organ
  alike would pass the first half and fail the second. A mechanics check on the busy box
  showed exactly that: head ×2.16, other organs ×1.44.

## Registered predictions (from ROADMAP §5; scored, not gated)

- v0 at 2048 is 0.70-0.90 of T0's desk, which is 24-31 tok/s.
- v0 at 512 is 1.05-1.20 × v0 at 2048.

## Cost and session

**Cost (ESTIMATE):**
- A1b: ~25-35 min.
- S0: ~1 h, which is about 2.5 min of planted pairs, 40 min of rate rounds and 15 min of organ
  processes. A 4096 process is ~2.5 min, most of it the sequential fill.
- Together: **~1.5-1.75 h of empty machine** (owner action, ROADMAP §6 rule 7), after the A4 →
  A6 chain and the A4 verdict.


## What this cannot say

- **One box, one artifact.** The scope is this 3600X and T0. Other machines are Phase 3.
- **Decode only.** The fill is sequential; a batched prefill would change the time to first
  token, not v0.
- **The organ times are wall laps on the main thread.** Each organ's time includes its slowest
  worker and its barrier.
  - With `--organs` there are two more jobs per MoE layer, so the breakdown describes that
    variant. The overhead row says by how much it differs.
  - The breakdown is a work order, not a per-kernel roofline: A1 is the roofline.
