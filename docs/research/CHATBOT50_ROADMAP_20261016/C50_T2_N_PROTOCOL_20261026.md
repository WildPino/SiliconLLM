# C50-T2 option N protocol: do new experts, distilled from F0 and attached to v0, add what one more pass does not?

26 October 2026. Registered before any training session of this cell runs and before any of its evaluations exists.
It is step 2 of decision C (§12, 24 October), on the base step 1 left: v0 = T0 + A1.
- **The choice:** "1: distillazione", after the trained stitch's STOP ([result](C50_T2_TSTITCH_RESULT_20261026.md)).
- **Option N:** new experts of T0's size, trained from scratch with F0 as teacher, in the gated additive branch of the
  [design](C50_T2_DESIGN_20261025.md). F0's knowledge reaches v0 through the distillation, not through F0's weights.

## Option X is frozen, with its state recorded

Two cells on X without a pass. Under the frozen-organ rule, X stops here and the pipeline moves on.

| What was measured | State |
| --- | --- |
| Linear stitch (ridge), every v0 layer to every F0 layer | FAIL: no layer stitches. About half of F0's routing (top-8 overlap 0.48-0.67), little of its experts' output (cosine 0.40-0.79; 0.15-0.28 at matched depth) |
| Trained stitch (affine and affine + MLP, on F0's routed output), depth-matched | STOP (the planted positive missed on 2 of 19 layers). No layer reaches the bar; training closes 20-53% of the cosine gap, 0.10-0.41 short; the routing does not move; affine = MLP |
| Data | the trained maps gain 0.062 cosine per doubling of the fit documents (median): 32-128× the documents for the middle layers, by a two-point extrapolation |

**What would reopen X:** a map family not tried (deep, or fed by several of v0's layers), or a dump on far more
documents. Neither is planned.

## Question

At v0's speed, can new experts attached to v0 learn from F0 what v0 does not already know, beyond what one more pass of
step 1's own recipe gives, without costing v0's facts?

## What trains

**Arm N** (`c50_t2_n.py`). At each MoE layer i = 1..19, with h the MoE input (v0's ffn_norm of the residual):

    y = MoE_v0(h) + relu(z_k*(h)) · E_k*(h),    z = R h + c,    k* = argmax(z + sel)

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| v0 | frozen, from its own GGUF (`t1_a1_q4_k_m.gguf`, sha256 `e490f803…`). Its router, top-8 and weights are unchanged: **nothing competes for v0's slots** (S4 arm (a)'s failure) |
| E_1..E_4 | 4 new SwiGLU experts per layer, of T0's size (2048 → 512 → 2048), trained from scratch; gate and up random (seeded), **down zero** |
| Router R, c | 4 rows per layer, trained; the pick is top-1 |
| Gate | the ReLU of the picked expert's router logit: where it is 0, the expert is not read. No fire cost: the fire rate is a measured output |
| sel | a selection-only bias per expert, after each step sel += 0.01 · sign(mean load − load): the base's aux-loss-free rule at S4 arm (a)'s rate, so the 4 experts share the picks |

- **Trainable:** 19 × (4 × 3,145,728 + 4 × 2,048 + 4) = **239,231,052** parameters, about arm (a)'s 174 M.
- **Stored capacity added:** +1.56% of v0's routed experts (15.30 B). This cell tests whether attached experts learn
  F0's knowledge at all. It does not test a 2× capacity: that needs more experts than Adam fits on T4 × 2.
- **At step 0 the student is v0 bit for bit,** because the branch's output is multiplied by a zero down-projection.
  This is the planted control: each session's first act checks max |Δlogit| = 0 against v0's own forward, or the
  session is VOID.

**Target and recipe: step 1's, unchanged** (C50-T1 part 2b, `c50_t1_train.anchored`), with v0 as the anchor's base:
- at a position whose target y has p_v0(y) ≥ 0.5 and p_v0(y) ≥ p_F0(y), the target is v0's own distribution (the
  branch switched off, no gradient). This is the "v0 sure" signal of the earlier choice, used as a target;
- elsewhere F0's top-32 and the rest, the 33-bucket KL;
- S3 block 1, shards 0-469, one epoch in stored order, 8,192 tokens per step (1,240 steps); AdamW (0.9, 0.999), wd 0,
  lr 1e-4, 30 warm-up steps, cosine to 1e-5; clip 1.0; fp16 forward with fp32 trainables; seed 20261016;
- validation on shards 470-471 at step 0, every 250 steps and at the end: S4's KL to F0, the anchored KL, the anchored
  share and the fire rate.

**Control E2, the extra pass** (`c50_t1_train.py`, unchanged, with v0's GGUF): a new LoRA r48, anchored, on v0, with
the same data, order, recipe and seed. It is step 1's recipe applied once more.
- **Why this control:** v0 has already seen block 1 once. Any arm trained on it again could gain from the second pass
  alone. E2 measures that. N must beat both v0 and E2.
- **Why r48 and not a LoRA of N's count:** in S4, r48 and r496 recovered the same share (0.699 and 0.695). The LoRA's
  rank does not bind at this volume, so a 239 M LoRA would add cost, not a different control.

## Planted controls and feasibility, before this push

**CPU, on v0, at step 0** (`c50_t2_n.py --controls`, record `t2/n/controls/controls.json`): all pass (`results/c50_t2_n_controls.json`).
- **Identity:** on 48 tokens, the student against v0's own forward gives max |Δlogit| 0.0.
- **Gradients at step 0** reach D only (max 0.69); every other part gets exactly 0, as it must behind D = 0. With D
  set non-zero, every part (R, c, G, U, D) gets a gradient.
- **The anchor's switch:** with D non-zero, the anchor's p_v0(y) equals v0's forward (max difference 0.0), and the
  student differs from v0 (5.11).
- **The shuffle** moves the student (6.30).
- **Fire share at step 0:** 0.83-0.99 per layer. The largest of 4 random logits is above 0 at about 94% of tokens.

**T4 × 2, 20 steps of the real recipe** (task `t2mve`): `results/c50_t2_n_mve.json` (acct3, ~15 min, no checkpoint kept, no evaluation read).
- **Identity on the GPU:** max |Δlogit| 0.0 on 512 tokens.
- **Step time:** 38.0 s median, of which the anchor's pass is 8.0-8.8 s. A1 took 37.6 s on the same T4s.
- **Memory:** GPU peak 9.92 / 11.41 GiB of the T4s' 15; host 6.4 GiB of 30.
- **The loss follows the batches:** it rises and falls on the same steps as A1's first 20 (for example steps 11 and
  19), lower by 0.11-0.25 because v0 has seen them once.
- **The anchored share is 0.12-0.19** against A1's 0.24-0.31 on T0. v0 sits closer to F0, so p_v0(y) ≥ p_F0(y) holds
  less often.
- **The busiest expert takes 0.30-0.36 of the picks** (0.25 is even), and the fire share is 0.93.

**Verdict script** (`c50_t2_n_verdict.py --selftest`): 8 of 8 planted cases give their outcome, including a gain that
E2 shares (FAIL), a gain that survives the shuffle (NOT PASSED) and a gain carried by 3 of 36 documents (FAIL, its
CI95 reaches 0).

## Evaluation (PyTorch, T4 × 2)

`c50_t2_n_eval.py`, S4's evaluator with v0's GGUF as the base. Four evaluations:

| Arm | Model |
| --- | --- |
| v0 | v0 alone |
| N | v0 + the trained branch |
| N-shuffle | the same checkpoint, with each layer's expert e moved to slot (e + 1) mod 4: every token reaches another expert of its own layer at the same gate value |
| E2 | v0 + E2's LoRA |

**Panels:**
- **docs:** S4's 36 documents. R(X) = (bits_T0 − bits_X) / (S2's T0 → F0 gap), as in S4; bits_T0 is MVE-5's
  PyTorch T0.
- **Facts:** C50-T1 addendum 2's fresh items, whole: nq 600, tqa 600, mmlu 566, arc 572. They were read once, to
  close step 1. No choice in this cell uses them.
- **Rest:** A4's gsm8k and chat panels, whole, plain draws.

The remove control is v0 itself (the branch off is v0 exactly, by the identity check).

**Each record holds** the GGUF's and the weights' sha256, and for N the share of (token, layer) pairs where the branch
fired, per panel and per layer.

## Rule (applied by `c50_t2_n_verdict.py`, from the records only)

| Gate | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary** | R(N) − R(v0) ≥ 0.05 **and** R(N) − R(E2) ≥ 0.05, each with its CI95 (paired document bootstrap, 10,000 draws) above 0 |
| **Facts** | on each of nq, tqa, mmlu and arc (fresh), N is not worse than v0 by A4's paired rule |
| **Rest** | on gsm8k and chat, N is not worse than v0 by the same rule |
| **Shuffle** | R(N-shuffle) − R(v0) ≤ (R(N) − R(v0)) / 2: the gain lives in what each expert holds |
| **Training guard** | N and E2 both end with an anchored validation KL below their step-0 value; else INCONCLUSIVE |

**Why 0.05:** it is 15% of the 0.33 of the gap that v0 leaves, and 0.012 BPB on the documents. A smaller gain could
not pay for a new organ in the engine.

| Outcome | Condition | Next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PASS** ("quality PASS, invariance pending") | all gates | the branch goes to the engine (its bytes at the measured fire rate, S1's check) and to a larger K, with Adam's states off the GPU, toward the thesis's capacity |
| **NOT PASSED** | the primary holds, another gate fails | design review |
| **FAIL** | the primary fails | N is closed at block 1's volume. The choice returns. If E2 itself gains ≥ 0.05 over v0, more passes of the step-1 recipe become one option |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | the training guard fails | review |
| **STOP** | any check: a GGUF that is not v0, weights that are not the trainings', an incomplete panel, an identity check that is not 0 | no verdict, review |

**Reported, not gated:**
- R of every arm, and E2 − v0;
- every panel against v0 and against E2;
- the fire rate per panel and per layer, and the validation curves.

## Bytes per token (DESK-MODEL, priced before any run)

One T0 expert in v0's Q4_K_M (gate and up Q4_K, down Q6_K) is 1.77-2.04 MB, 1.90 MB on average (from the GGUF's
tensors).
- **With fire rate p over 19 layers:** Δbytes ≈ p × 36.1 MB per token, plus 0.31 MB for the 4 router rows per layer
  in BF16 (read at every token).
- **Against v0's 867.3 MB:** +0.04% at p = 0, +2.1% at p = 0.5, +4.2% at p = 1.
- **So the speed cost is small at any fire rate.** That is why no fire cost is trained: the gate exists for exact
  zeros, not for bytes. An engine port of the branch is not built in this cell.

## What this cannot say

- **One seed, one recipe, one K.** No rate or K is tuned.
- **+1.56% stored capacity.** A FAIL closes N at this size and volume, not distillation into experts in general. A
  PASS shows that attached experts learn from F0. It does not show the 2× capacity of the thesis.
- **The same 9.5 M teacher positions v0 learned from.** The cell cannot separate "the data is spent" from "the
  experts cannot use it", except through E2.
- **36 documents and four fresh fact panels** judge it. The panels are proxies (S2).
- **Quality only.** The bytes above are a desk price, and no engine runs the branch yet.
