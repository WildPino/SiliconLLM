# C50-S4 design: the falsifier at m ≈ 2, before its protocol

22 October 2026. Cell S4 of the [CHATBOT50 roadmap](ROADMAP.md) §5, Phase 1. This note fixes the design decisions,
the arm sizes and the feasibility checks that come before the S4 protocol. The protocol, with its gate numbers and
predictions, is pushed after those checks and before any arm is trained. No arm has been trained.

## What S4 asks (ROADMAP §5)

On the frozen T0 base, with matched data, does added capacity beat a LoRA control with the same trainable-parameter
count on S2's donor-gap panel?

- **Arms:** (a) extra routed experts with new router rows; (b) a conditional memory bank (deterministic addressing,
  values built offline from the teacher and frozen, a trained read-out and gate); (c) a LoRA control.
- **Gate:** (a) or (b) beats (c) by a margin frozen in the protocol, for example recovering ≥ 20% of the
  donor-minus-T0 gap; no regression on the general panel; on the same checkpoint, removing, shuffling or masking the
  capacity erases the gain; the engine passes S1's invariance gate on the trained artifact.
- **Stop:** if neither (a) nor (b) beats (c) beyond the margin, capacity transfer is closed **at that budget**.

## Inputs already measured

- **T0 = P0** (`ling-mini-2.0-a810f64-q4_k_m.gguf`, `44de7315…`): 20 layers, width 2048, 16 query and 4 KV heads of
  128, layer 0 dense (FFN 5120), layers 1-19 with 256 routed experts (3 × 2048 × 512 = 3.15 M parameters each, 8 per
  token, 8 groups of which 4 are kept, sigmoid scores, weights × 2.5) plus one shared expert of the same size.
  16.3 B parameters, ~1.1 B active per token without the embedding.
- **Teacher signal:** S3 block 1, 10,006,142 positions of corpus v1 with F0's top-32 log-probabilities (GPU path,
  addendum 2), 494 shards, the three parts at 33.33% each.
- **Target panels (S2):** the donor-gap panel, 409 items F0 gets right and T0 wrong (nq 98, tqa 117, mmlu 79, arc 105,
  gsm8k 10) and 71 of the 72 FIT documents (F0 at least 0.05 BPB below T0). On the documents F0 − M0 = −0.235 BPB and
  T0 − M0 = −0.005. The general panel is the rest of S2's items plus A4's chat panel.
- **S1:** speed invariance at m2 and m4 holds in both router formats; S4 uses the BF16 router.
- **F0 is not a source of experts.** It has 32 layers of width 4096 and experts of 4096 × 1024: its experts do not fit
  T0's residual stream without trained maps at every layer, and they would change the bytes per token that S1 priced.

## MVE-1 (done, 22 October): T0 in PyTorch, from T0's own tensors

`c50_s4_t0_torch.py` builds T0 as a PyTorch forward from the GGUF tensors, dequantized with the pinned llama.cpp's
`gguf-py`, from the semantics of the model card's code (written anew, the remote code is not run). On the first 512
tokens of 4 FIT documents (2,044 positions), CPU, fp32:

| Comparison | mean dlogp | mean \|dlogp\| | top-1 agreement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| PyTorch T0 against P0 (A4's llama.cpp scores) | −0.001 | 0.077 | 97.1% |
| PyTorch T0 against BF16 (A4) | −0.013 | 0.132 | 95.6% |
| P0 against BF16, the same positions (the yardstick) | −0.012 | 0.145 | 95.4% |

The PyTorch T0 is closer to P0 than P0 is to BF16, and as far from BF16 as P0. Training can therefore see T0's exact
weights rather than BF16 or another quantization. Record: `results/c50_s4_mve1.json`. Wall 1,056 s on the CPU,
dominated by dequantizing every layer per document.

## Decisions

1. **The base is T0's exact weights, frozen.** The training forward is `c50_s4_t0_torch.py`. Nothing of T0 is
   trained in any arm.
2. **Training runs on Kaggle T4 x2** (more than 1 h of training goes to T4; fp16 on Turing). T0's quantized blocks
   (9.9 GB) stay on the GPUs, layers 0-9 on one and 10-19 on the other, and each layer is dequantized to fp16 when it is
   used, forward and backward. Its dequantized fp16 copy (32.6 GB) would not fit the two GPUs, and the session's ~30 GB
   of host RAM does not hold it either.
3. **Loss and data, the same for every arm.** The 33-bucket KL from the teacher to the student: F0's top-32
   probabilities plus one bucket for the rest of the vocabulary. Block 1's shards 0-469 are the training split; shards
   470-493 (~0.5 M positions) are a validation slice for the training curves, never a gate. One epoch, the same order,
   batch, schedule and seed for every arm.
4. **Arm (a), extra routed experts at m2.** Each MoE layer goes from 256 to 536 experts with S1's m2 layout, so the
   routing groups stay equal (32 originals and 35 new slots per group).
   - **The full m2 cannot be trained here:** 5,320 new experts are 16.7 B parameters, ~270 GB of Adam state.
   - **A new slot holds a frozen copy of an expert of its group plus a trained rank-4 delta** on its three matrices.
     Its router row is a trained copy of its source's row, perturbed so the copies get picked from the start (S1's arm
     E spread its picks over the copies this way). The original 256 rows stay frozen.
   - **Trainable:** deltas 163.43 M + new router rows 10.90 M + new expert biases = **174.33 M**.
   - **At step 0 the model computes T0's function** up to which copy is picked (a copy with a zero delta is its source).
   - **Stored, this is the m2 that S1 timed** (the copies), plus the deltas: 61 KB in fp16 per new expert, read only
     when that expert is picked. Its bytes per token are priced before the run (rule 9). **The learned capacity is the
     deltas, not the copies, and every report says so.**
5. **Arm (b), a conditional memory bank.**
   - **Address:** the trigram that ends at the current token, falling back to its bigram on a miss; a row exists for
     every trigram and bigram seen at least twice in the training split.
   - **Value, frozen:** F0's mean top-32 distribution after that n-gram, over the training split, mapped into T0's
     space through T0's own output embedding: v = Σ_k p_k W_out[k].
   - **Read-out:** at two sites (after the attention of layers 4 and 12), a trained key map, value map and scalar gate.
     **Trainable: 2 × 8.39 M = 16.78 M.**
   - Its stored size is set by the n-gram counts of the training split. It is reported as m, and may be below 2 at
     block 1.
6. **Arm (c), the LoRA control: one per capacity arm, matched to its trainable count.** LoRA on the always-on modules
   (fused QKV, attention output, the shared expert, layer 0's dense FFN), on all 20 layers: rank 496 = 174.47 M for
   (a) (+0.08%); rank 48 = 16.88 M for (b) (+0.6%).
7. **Evaluation is in the same PyTorch forward for every arm and for T0.** Arm (b) cannot run in llama.cpp, so the
   S2 tools cannot score the arms. Each panel follows S2's rule: generation lengths, the likelihood rule, and the
   short-answer, GSM8K and chat checks of `c50_a4_checks.py`. Only the generator changes.
   - **T0 in PyTorch is the baseline inside the same instrument.** The gap's size comes from S2: per document, T0 − F0
     in BPB (llama.cpp).
8. **Gate form.** The numbers are frozen in the protocol after the feasibility checks, before any training.
   - **Primary:** the recovered share on the 71 documents, R(X) = (BPB_T0 − BPB_X) / mean gap, for each capacity arm
     against its own LoRA control.
   - **Secondary:** the 409 donor-gap items (not worse than the control); the general panel (no regression against
     T0).
   - **Controls on the same checkpoint:** remove the capacity (route nothing to the new slots / gate the bank to 0);
     shuffle it (deltas permuted among copies of a group / values permuted among rows of similar count); mask 50% of
     the bank's rows at equal lookup count.
   - **The engine check of S1** on the trained artifact is for the winner only. Until it is done, a quality PASS is
     labelled "quality PASS, invariance pending".

## Feasibility checks before the protocol

| Check | What it decides | Where | Budget |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MVE-1** | the PyTorch T0 is T0 | CPU, local | done |
| **MVE-2** | the GPU dequantization of Q4_K and Q6_K in PyTorch is equal to `gguf-py`, bit for bit; the fp16 forward on T4 stays within MVE-1's yardstick against P0 | Kaggle T4 x2 | ≤ 1 GPU-h |
| **MVE-3** | memory and throughput of one training step for each arm (a, b, c at both ranks), 200 steps each, on block 1's first shards; the S4 run's time | Kaggle T4 x2 | ≤ 3 GPU-h |
| **MVE-4** | the bank's rows and bytes from the training split's n-gram counts (sets arm (b)'s m) | CPU, local | ~1 h |

## T4 work, announced (rule 7)

- **Reason:** S4's training (ROADMAP §5); first MVE-2 and MVE-3.
- **Budget:** ≤ 4 GPU-h for the checks; the S4 run itself (4 trainings of one epoch on ~9.5 M positions, plus the
  evaluations) is sized by MVE-3 and fixed in the protocol, within the granted 90 h per week.
- **Uploads:** T0's GGUF (9.9 GB), block 1 (2.7 GB) and the code, to one account; each file is checked against its
  hash on the session's side.
- **Stop rule:** a check that fails its criterion stops the line for a design review (rule 3), not a retry.

## MVE-2 criterion, fixed before the run (22 October)

- **Code:** `c50_s4_dequant.py` (Q4_K and Q6_K in PyTorch, a port of the pinned `gguf-py`), `c50_s4_t0_gpu.py` (T0's
  quantized blocks on the two GPUs, layers 0-9 and the embedding on the first, layers 10-19 and the output head on the
  second, dequantized to fp16 when used; residual stream, norms and router in fp32; fp16 reduced-precision reduction
  off), the kernel `kaggle/c50_s4_kernel.py` and the driver `c50_s4_kaggle.py`. The verdict is
  `c50_s4_mve2_verdict.py`, from the runner's record.
- **Dequantization:** all 159 quantized tensors of T0 (Q4_K and Q6_K) give, on the GPU, the same fp32 bytes as the
  pinned `gguf-py` (sha256 per tensor, computed locally from `gguf-py`). On the local CPU the PyTorch port already
  matches on every tensor.
- **Fidelity:** the fp16 GPU forward on MVE-1's 2,044 positions, against P0: mean |dlogp| ≤ 0.1448 and top-1
  agreement ≥ 0.9535, the P0-against-BF16 row of MVE-1 on the same positions.
- **Also recorded, no criterion:** load time, memory after load and at peak, the dequantization time of one MoE layer
  on each GPU, and the forward time per document. These feed MVE-3.
- **A failed criterion stops the line for a design review**, not a retry.
