## Conduct of the run

- **Sessions:** trainings a 2 sessions (18.9 h), b 1 (8.96 h) after a VOID session (2.75 h, container death,
  `51a70c3`), c48 2 (10.3 h), c496 2 (11.49 h); nine evaluations of ~1 h each, plus one VOID evaluation
  (`eval-a_shuffle`, container death after < 31 min, rerun, `206a5db`). About 66 of the 75 session-hours announced.
- **Every resumed or evaluation session** mounted the previous kernel's output; its server metadata was checked
  before it ran, and each evaluation log shows the weights path and mode it was given and ends with `DONE`.
- **Two container deaths, both unexplained**, both with an empty log and no files; neither recurred on rerun.

## What this says, and what it does not

- **It says** that at ~9.5 M positions of F0's top-32 logits, with one recipe, neither added routed capacity nor a
  conditional memory bank beats a LoRA with the same trainable count on the S4 documents. The control is not a weak
  one: rank 48 already recovers 70% of the gap, and rank 496 adds nothing (0.6946 vs 0.6992), so the LoRA is not
  limited by its rank at this volume.
- **It does not say** that capacity cannot help at a larger volume, with another recipe, or on knowledge that the
  documents do not test. One seed per arm; 36 documents and 206 items.
- **Post-hoc only:** the LoRA's document gain comes with closed-book regressions on nq and tqa against T0, which
  arm (b) does not show. This was not registered; it is a lead, not a finding.
- **S5 has no winner to run,** so Phase 1's "quality growth (S4, S5)" exit condition cannot be met as designed. The
  next step is a decision (ROADMAP §12, this date).
