### Does any of this family reach 2-bit or ternary post-training, and at what cost?

**Yes, but with an important split that matters for S1:**

- The **canonical rotation-invariance family (QuaRot, SpinQuant)** as published tops out at **4-bit** weight+activation+KV, and reports that as a near-lossless floor (≤0.5 PPL, ~99% zero-shot retention at 70B). Neither paper, in the sections surfaced by this search, claims a 2-bit or ternary result. **QuaRot explicitly frames 4-bit as the achievement, not a waypoint.**
- **QuIP/QuIP#**, a sibling family using the same incoherence-processing idea (random/Hadamard orthogonal transform) but paired with **vector quantization** (lattice codebooks) rather than scalar rounding, reaches **2-bit and is the first PTQ family to do so credibly**. This is architecturally close to what S1 would need but is a heavier machinery than "rotate then round" — it needs a codebook, not just a scale, which is a real divergence from the engine's ternary-LUT kernel contract (§8.G) and would need its own kernel, not a drop-in.
- **A newer 2026 wave (TWLA, ScaleQ-1.58, and the PT²-LLM/PTQ1.61 family discussed under Q2)** explicitly targets **1.58-bit / ternary** using rotation-like reshaping ("tri-modal shaping") plus calibration. This is the neighborhood the mandate's own §11 flagged as "most promising" and it is real — multiple independent 2025–2026 papers converge on rotation-assisted ternary PTQ as a live research direction, not vaporware. **But every number from this 2026 cluster in this survey came from a single search-result summary, not a verified primary-source fetch of the exact table.** Treat the specific percentages above as **leads to verify**, not settled numbers, before they inform a pre-registration threshold.

### Applicability to S1

- QuaRot's data-free 6/8-bit round-to-nearest path and its rotation-fusion mechanism are **squarely inside S1's "deterministic algebraic transform" category** — a fixed Hadamard rotation, fused into adjacent weights, with no gradient step anywhere, is about as clean a "transformation-only" operation as exists in this literature.
- QuaRot/SpinQuant's 4-bit configurations add a **GPTQ-style calibration pass** — still inside S1 as "forward-only calibration," provided the calibration truly stays forward-only (GPTQ itself does a layer-local closed-form solve via Hessian inversion, not gradient descent — this is the "layer-wise local optimization" grey zone the mandate names explicitly and asks to be budgeted/declared separately).
- **SpinQuant's Cayley-optimized rotation is the one item in this table that sits closest to the S1 boundary and should be labeled as such explicitly**: it is a genuine (small) optimization loop with gradients, even though those gradients never touch the donor's own weight tensors. Per the mandate's own operational definition (§S1), this is legitimate as a "separately declared local solve" but must be reported under that heading, with iteration count, wall time, and objective stated up front — not folded into "data-free rotation" language.
- QuIP#'s lattice-codebook step is a **closed-form nearest-lattice-point search**, not gradient-based — also inside S1, but it changes the kernel contract (needs codebook lookup, not the engine's pshufb ternary LUT), which is an engine-generalization cost (§8.G), not a quality cost.

## Q2 — Post-training ternary degradation: the actual numbers, and where the mandate's "~2×" claim likely comes from

**The mandate's claim (§8.C, §2 of the master prompt):** "roughly ~2× perplexity at 7B–70B in at least one published study" for naive post-training ternarization. **This survey could not find a single study that cleanly supports "~2×" as the number for *naive* (round-to-nearest / plain GPTQ-with-ternary-grid) ternarization. The evidence found says the real picture is bimodal, and the mandate's number sits in neither mode cleanly — it is closer to describing a sophisticated method's residual gap than the naive failure mode.**

### What was actually found, by method sophistication

**Reading this table honestly:**

1. **The mandate's "~2×" figure does not match the naive-PTQ failure mode found here, which is catastrophic (order(s) of magnitude), not a doubling.** If "naive" is the operative word — round-to-nearest or plain GPTQ pushed onto a 3-level ternary grid with no rotation, no activation-guided calibration, no scale migration — the measured outcome in every source found this pass is **model-destroying**, not "roughly 2×." This is a **flag: the mandate's own number appears to be an underestimate of how bad the truly naive path is**, which if anything *strengthens* its own conclusion ("assume the naive path is broken") but for the wrong reason if quoted as "~2×" in a pre-registration.
2. **"~2×" is in the right neighborhood only for methods that already include real machinery** (OneBit's 1.80× ratio, which is a trained/distilled method, not PTQ at all). None of the pure-PTQ methods surveyed land near 2×: they are either near-lossless (PT²-LLM, ~1.04–1.08×) or catastrophic (naive RTN/GPTQ, >18× to effectively infinite).
3. **This is genuinely good news for S1 if PT²-LLM's numbers hold up under independent verification**: a training-free, calibration-only, 128-sample method reporting 1.04–1.08× perplexity ratio at 7B/13B/70B on LLaMA-2 would mean the "naive path is broken, assume degradation" framing in the mandate is **too pessimistic** for the current state of the art, not too optimistic — the opposite direction from what §8.C assumes. **This is exactly the kind of contradiction the mandate's own reading rules (§0.3) require reporting to the Architect immediately, not footnoting.** Recommend Stage −1 use PT²-LLM (or TWLA, if its numbers verify) as the "best calibration-only method" rather than assuming the field caps out at ~2×.
4. **Caveat that cuts the other way:** PT²-LLM and TWLA's numbers came from single-pass PDF/search extractions in this session, done by an automated summarizer, not by this Researcher reading the raw tables directly. Given this project's own "plausible artefact" law (§6.3) and its explicit instruction to distrust automated tool summaries, **these specific ratios (1.04×, 1.08×) should be treated as a strong lead, not a citable fact, until the Adapter or a Controller re-derives them from the primary PDF table directly** — WikiText2 perplexity numbers this close to lossless for *ternary* weights, with no training, would be a genuinely surprising result relative to the ~2018–2023 quantization literature's general expectation that sub-4-bit is where things get hard, and surprising results are exactly where a "plausible artefact" is most likely (mismatched eval harness, decontamination issue, or a report of a cherry-picked layer-subset).

### BitNet b1.58 and Spectra/TriLM: confirmed QAT/from-scratch, and what they say about the gap

- **BitNet b1.58 (original, and the 2B4T technical report, arXiv 2504.12285)** is confirmed **QAT with shadow weights**: "The canonical quantization-aware training pipeline replaces each linear/projection layer with a BitLinear module... Shadow weights (16-bit/bf16) are retained throughout training, but only quantized weights are kept for inference." This is **pretraining-time** quantization-awareness, not PTQ, and the 2B4T report itself explicitly benchmarks against **PTQ baselines (GPTQ/AWQ on Qwen2.5-1.5B)** and reports BitNet ahead on average score (55.01 vs 52.15) — i.e., **BitNet's own paper treats "QAT beats PTQ at matched bit-budget" as a headline finding**, which directly supports the mandate's instinct that PTQ ternarization is the harder path, even though the specific magnitude ("~2×") doesn't hold up as stated.
- **Spectra / TriLM (arXiv 2407.12327, plus Spectra-1.1 arXiv 2506.23025)** is confirmed **from-scratch pretraining** across FloatLM/QuantLM/TriLM variants, 99M–3.9B params, up to 1.2T tokens for Spectra-1.1. TriLM is reported to **match FloatLM at the same parameter count using fewer bits**, and to benefit more from data scaling than parameter scaling. **This is a pretraining-time result and says nothing directly about the PTQ-vs-QAT gap for a donor that is already trained** — it is evidence that ternary-from-scratch works at these scales, not evidence about converting an existing fp16 donor. Do not blur the two, per the mandate's own instruction.
- **The crux, stated plainly:** the mandate is right that QAT (BitNet, Spectra/TriLM) and PTQ are different regimes and must not be conflated. It is **wrong, on the evidence found this pass, about the specific "~2×" naive-PTQ number** — naive PTQ is far worse than 2× degradation, while the best 2025–2026 calibration-only PTQ methods claim to be far better than 2× (near-lossless), which is the more important and more surprising fact for this project's Stage −1 design.

## Q3 — Attention→SSM conversion and the healing budget

### Mamba in the Llama (Wang, Paliotta, May, Rush, Dao — arXiv 2408.15237, NeurIPS 2024)

**(a) Initialization mapping — direct quote from the paper, cross-checked across two independent fetches of different arXiv versions (v1 and v4):**

> "Weights, in orange, are initialized from the Transformer (Linear projections for Q, K, and V are initialized using linear projection for C, B, and X respectively)."

I.e.: **donor W_Q → SSM's C projection; donor W_K → SSM's B projection; donor W_V → SSM's X (input) projection.** The output projection W_O carries over to the SSM block's output reconstruction. **The SSM's own new parameters — the discretization step Δ and the diagonal state matrix A — are newly initialized, not derived from any donor tensor**; they have no attention-side analogue. This is the mapping the mandate's §8.B.2 asks to be stated explicitly and to flag which parts are principled vs analogy: **the Q/K/V→C/B/X correspondence is dimensionally exact (same shapes) but the correspondence is a design choice, not a derived equivalence** — attention's C/B/X roles are not mathematically identical to what Q/K/V compute, they are being *repurposed* into a differently-shaped computation (linear recurrence vs quadratic attention) that happens to accept matrices of the same size. Treat "principled" as "shape-compatible initialization," not "provably equivalent."

**(b) Healing budget — sources disagree, report both:**

- One fetch (v1) reported: **"less than five days on 8×80GB A100... 20 billion tokens across the three-stage pipeline."**
- A second, more careful fetch (v4) of the same paper reported instead: **"The distillation phase takes eight days on 8×A100 and four days on 8×H100"** — describing the whole multi-stage pipeline (progressive distillation → SFT on GenQA/InfinityInstruct/OpenHermes-2.5 → DPO on UltraFeedback for Zephyr-derived models), not a single number.
- The GitHub repo (jxiw/MambaInLlama) states simply: **"you only need 8×80G A100 (with very limited resources) and run for 3 to 4 days to reproduce our results."**
- **Net: the healing budget for one hybrid checkpoint is in the range of ~3–8 days on an 8×A100 (or mixed A100/H100) node — i.e., roughly 600–1,500 A100-GPU-hours per checkpoint.** This is **far outside a single Kaggle T4/P100 9-hour session and outside the ~90 GPU-hours/week quota across all three accounts even summed over multiple weeks** — it would take 7–15+ weeks of the full quota with zero margin, which is exactly the "7 weeks of compute" the Owner explicitly ruled out in S1. **This is the load-bearing number for the whole §4 tension and it should be flagged to the Architect as confirming, numerically, that the published attention→SSM healing recipe is incompatible with S1 as stated** — not a surprise given the mandate already suspected this, but now with an actual GPU-hour range attached instead of "billions of tokens" as a vague gesture.
- **Token budget, separately: "20 billion tokens" was reported in only one of the two fetches and not corroborated elsewhere in this pass — treat as unverified pending a primary-source re-read of the paper's Section 5.**

**(c) Initialization-ONLY ablation — the single most valuable number the mandate asked for, and it does not appear to exist in this paper.**

Two independent WebFetch passes were run specifically hunting for this number. The first pass returned a fabricated-looking table ("Table 7 Left: pure Mamba PPL=3.36, hybrid PPL=2.09, init-only") which does not match the structure of what a "worse without training" number should look like (perplexity *lower* than any healed baseline reported elsewhere in the paper, which is implausible) — **this is flagged as a likely hallucination by the automated summarizer, per this project's own "plausible artefact" law, and should not be trusted or cited.** The second, more careful pass explicitly re-checked and reported:

> "I cannot find a table or sentence that reports model quality metrics using only the initialization step with zero subsequent distillation, fine-tuning, or training. The ablation studies that examine initialization effects (Table 7 and Table 8) all measure performance after training has occurred."

**Verdict: not located.** This paper's own ablations (comparing frozen-MLP vs unfrozen variants, per-stage progressive distillation) do not appear to isolate zero-healing quality. If this number exists in the paper, it was not surfaced by two fetch passes in this session and needs a direct read of Tables 7–8 by a human or a dedicated fetch targeting exactly those tables/pages. **This is the finding to report loudest to the Adapter: the paper the mandate names as the key precedent does not appear to publish the one number (§4's stage-2a screening depends on exactly this kind of local-fidelity-without-healing measurement) that would tell you whether initialization alone is viable** — meaning stage 2a genuinely has to be measured fresh, it cannot be estimated from this paper's own reported ablations.

**(d) Attention fraction retained:** the paper tests **50%, 25%, 12.5%, and 0%** attention retention, selected by **progressive halving** ("keep every 2 layers, distill, then every 4, and continue") — i.e., a fixed schedule, not a per-layer importance criterion. **This directly matters for §8.B.1's open question ("is there a measurable per-layer criterion"): Mamba-in-the-Llama does NOT use one — it uses uniform stride, which is exactly the kind of "control arm" the mandate's §4/§8.B closure criteria ask for as a baseline to beat, not as the final answer.**

**(e) Headline numbers (Llama-3-8B, 50% attention hybrid, cross-checked once, moderate confidence):** MT-Bench 8.00 (original) → 7.35 (hybrid); AlpacaEval-LC 22.90% → 29.61% (hybrid *higher*, unusual and worth independent verification before quoting); MMLU zero-shot 57.81% for the hybrid (original not stated in the same extraction). **These specific numbers came from one fetch pass; recommend re-verifying the AlpacaEval improvement specifically, since a hybrid model outscoring its own teacher on an eval is the kind of result that deserves suspicion per the mandate's own §6 law ("treat with suspicion proportional to how much you want it to be true").**

### MOHAWK (Bick, Li, Xing, Kolter, Gu — arXiv 2408.10189, NeurIPS 2024)

**(a) Initialization / method:** MOHAWK is explicitly a **3-stage progressive distillation**, not a single closed-form initialization:
1. **Matrix orientation** — align the student SSM's induced token-mixing matrix to the teacher attention matrix by minimizing Frobenius distance (this stage is the closest analogue to a "layer-local solve" under S1 — it is a per-layer, gradient-based fit against a fixed teacher target, not end-to-end).
2. **Hidden-state alignment** — match block outputs (attention block vs SSM block) via L2 distance.
3. **Weight transfer + end-to-end knowledge distillation** — remaining non-mixer weights (MLP/norm/embeddings) copied from the teacher, then the **full student is fine-tuned end-to-end** with a logit-distillation loss. **This final stage is explicitly end-to-end gradient training and is outside S1 as a primary path**, exactly as the mandate's §4 already anticipates.

**(b) Token budget, confirmed via direct abstract fetch:** **Phi-Mamba (fully replaces attention, 0% retained): 3 billion tokens. Hybrid Phi-Mamba: 5 billion tokens.** Both figures came directly from the paper's own abstract text (fetched verbatim, high confidence). Hardware: 8×A100 mentioned in secondary sources but not pinned to an exact wall-clock/GPU-hour figure in this pass. **3–5B tokens is dramatically cheaper than Mamba-in-the-Llama's implied budget, and is at least within plausible reach of a multi-session Kaggle campaign if throughput is favorable — this is worth flagging as the more S1-compatible of the two named precedents, even though its final stage is still end-to-end distillation and therefore still technically outside S1's primary path.**

**(c) Initialization-only (Stage 1 only) ablation:** search results state qualitatively that **"each phase is highly beneficial... attempting to directly distill Phi-1.5 (i.e., Phase 3 alone) substantially underperforms the full MOHAWK method"** — this confirms the *reverse* comparison (skipping stages 1–2 hurts), which is suggestive that stage-by-stage matching matters, but **no exact perplexity/accuracy number for "Stage 1 only, no stages 2–3" was located** despite three separate fetch attempts (PDF fetch failed to parse cleanly; the project blog post explicitly stated it does not include a Stage-1-only table; a targeted search did not surface one either). **Verdict: not located**, same as Mamba-in-the-Llama. **Both of the mandate's two named precedents fail to publish the exact ablation the mandate most wants** — this is a pattern, not a one-off, and probably means the field genuinely has not asked this question the way this project needs it asked (which is itself the finding: nobody has seriously tried "closed-form/local-solve only, no healing" per §4.i, exactly as the mandate speculated).

**(d) Attention fraction:** Phi-Mamba is **fully converted (0% attention retained)**; **Hybrid Phi-Mamba retains an unspecified fraction — not pinned down in this pass** (the blog post that would likely state this was "Part I" of a series and explicitly deferred hybrid details to a later, unfetched part). Flag as an open gap.

### Reading this table for §8.A's eligibility predicate

- **Every model in this table with a confirmed downloadable status is Apache-2.0 or an explicitly-permissive named license** (Jamba's own license, NVIDIA's Nemotron license, MiniMax's license) — **except** none found here are outright non-permissive/research-only, which is mildly encouraging for the licence axis, but MiniMax's "cannot use outputs to improve another LLM" clause is exactly the kind of redistribution restriction the mandate's §8.A.6 asks to be recorded without giving legal advice — **recorded here, not adjudicated.**
- **Vocabulary sizes cluster in two bands**: the "traditional" 32K–65K band (Zamba2, Jamba) and the "modern large-vocab" 128K–200K band (Nemotron-H, Bamba, MiniMax). Per §5's head-arithmetic warning, **the 128K–200K band donors carry a genuinely heavy head** (at D≈4096–8192 the embedding/head matrix alone is hundreds of millions of params, matching the mandate's own worked example almost exactly) — this argues for prioritizing either a smaller-vocab hybrid donor (Zamba2, Jamba at 32–65K) for the first end-to-end trial, or accepting the head-compression axis (§8.H) as mandatory rather than optional for any large-vocab pick.
- **Attention fraction varies enormously across real released donors**: from Granite 4.0/Nemotron-H's **~8–10%** (comfortably "minority" under S3) to Qwen3-Next/MiniMax's **25%** (still minority, but a different regime) to Jamba's **12.5%** to Hunyuan-TurboS's **~5.5%**. **This is itself directly useful evidence for §8.B's "what is the fraction" question**: the field's own released hybrids converge on roughly 5–25% attention, never above ~25% in anything found here, and several production-scale (100B+) donors ship at under 10%. If this project's own measured retention-vs-fraction curve (§4, §8.B closure criterion) comes out needing significantly more than 25% attention to hit the S4 gates, that would be a real divergence from what the field's own trained-from-scratch hybrids apparently find sufficient — worth flagging either way once measured.
- **A hybrid donor does not, by itself, solve §4's tension.** These models were **trained from scratch as hybrids** (or MOHAWK/Mamba-in-Llama-distilled, in-house, with full healing budgets) — none of them were produced by the transformation-only recipe this project needs. Using one as a donor sidesteps the "convert attention to SSM" half of the problem (§4.ii-shaped: keep the SSM layers as SSM, keep the attention layers as attention, do not convert anything structurally) — but **it does not sidestep ternarizing an already-hybrid donor's weights (§8.C) or fitting its footprint (§S2)**, which remain open regardless of donor choice. This is the honest reading of "arrives half-converted": it removes one axis of risk (§4/§8.B), not all of them.

## Confidence and gaps — what could NOT be verified this pass

1. **QuIP and QuIP# exact perplexity tables at 2-bit and 3-bit** were not extracted to specific numbers — only qualitative claims ("first to achieve good 2-bit quantization," "3-bit beats 4-bit"). If Q1 becomes load-bearing for Stage −1's method selection, these need a direct table read.
2. **PT²-LLM's calibration-set size (128 WikiText samples) and its exact per-model perplexity table** came from a single automated PDF-fetch pass in this session. Given this project's own "plausible artefact" law and the surprising near-losslessness of the reported numbers, **this is the single highest-priority item to independently re-verify before it informs any pre-registration threshold.**
3. **PT-BitNet's exact baseline/comparison numbers** could not be verified against the primary paper — both the ScienceDirect and SSRN hosts returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch in this session. Only a secondary blog summary was reached. The paper is real (confirmed via PubMed indexing and a ScienceDirect DOI), but its numbers here are the weakest-sourced in the whole survey.
4. **The Mamba-in-the-Llama initialization-only ablation and the exact healing token/GPU-hour budget** — see Q3 above. Two fetch passes disagreed on the GPU-hour figure (5 days vs 8+4 days), and the "20 billion tokens" figure was reported only once and not corroborated. **A first automated fetch pass produced what looks like a fabricated number ("PPL=2.09 init-only") that a second, more careful pass did not confirm — this is exactly the "plausible artefact" failure mode this project's own law (§6.3) warns about, caught only because a second independent pass was run. Recommend: never trust a single automated-fetch extraction of a load-bearing number in this project without a second independent pass, per the pattern observed here.**
5. **MOHAWK's Stage-1-only (matrix-orientation-only) ablation number** — not located despite three attempts (direct PDF fetch failed to parse; project blog explicitly deferred it; targeted search did not surface it). Genuinely may not exist in published form.
6. **Several Q4 table cells** (Falcon-H1's per-size hidden dim/vocab, Bamba's exact attention-layer count within its 32 layers, Granite 4.0's vocab/hidden dim, Hunyuan-TurboS's downloadability) are marked "not extracted" or "not confirmed" and should be pulled from primary config files (`config.json` on Hugging Face) rather than search summaries before they feed footprint arithmetic (§5, §9 stage 1) — search-summary numbers are adequate for a shortlist, not for the arithmetic pass that eliminates candidates.
7. **No systematic check was done in this pass on decontamination**: none of the calibration-set or healing-corpus compositions reported above were checked against any of this project's own eval corpora. That check is unavoidable before Stage 5 (§8.L, §S4) but is out of scope for a literature survey.
8. **"Newer than 2026" successors**: TWLA (Jun 2026) and ScaleQ-1.58 (Aug 2026) are the newest items located, and Aug 2026 is close enough to today's date (2026-08-20) that this may not be the final word — a repeat search close to Stage −1's actual launch is worth doing given how fast this specific sub-field (rotation-assisted ternary PTQ) is moving.
