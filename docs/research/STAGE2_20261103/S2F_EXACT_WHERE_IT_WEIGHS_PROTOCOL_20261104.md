# Stage 2, part 1, test 3b: ternary experts, with the expert of highest weight read exact. PROTOCOL

2026-11-04. Ling-mini, quality only, no training. It runs after test 2b's chain, alone on the CPU.

## Question

Stage 1 (b)'s ternary experts cost −8.52 points. In each token, read the slot with the highest routing weight from the
original Q4_K_M and the others ternary. How much of −8.52 returns, and for how many milliseconds?

## Thesis line (rule 4)

On Ling-flash, one exact slot in every MoE layer costs +3.92 ms on the desk (29.45 ms stored against 13.77 ms ternary at
k = 4, over 4 slots). The room under 24.7 tok/s is 1.12 ms with test 2b's cell 1 (28% of the layers) and 2.29 ms with cell 2
(58%). All layers exact gives 23.1 or 23.7 tok/s: under the line. So the arm that decides is the one that fits the room.

- **Late layers carry it,** the reverse of DyMoE's reading on one layer alone.
- **Ling's routing is flat:** the top slot holds 26% of the normalized weight.
- **Noise floor:** a slot read from the model's own file differs from the kernel's by 5e-6 on average, yet top-1 against ref
  falls to 0.98 when it happens in early layers: rounding flips the routing. Top-1 here is not read below 0.02.
- **Test 3 left no guilty experts,** so the arm is the highest weight.

## Line (registered; `s2x_verdict.py`, selftest PASS, 10 cases)

- **EXACT WHERE IT WEIGHS CLOSES THE EXPERTS:** the judged arm fits (CI95 lower end ≥ −5, chat gate), about 7.3 of 8.52
  back, and beats its control.
- **HALF COMES BACK:** Δ ≥ −4.26 and it beats its control. Part 2 starts from there.
- **LITTLE COMES BACK:** otherwise. Part 2 stays a training.

Half is the price asked for the room: the arm spends all the milliseconds left. Control: margin against the random arm,
CI95 above 0.
