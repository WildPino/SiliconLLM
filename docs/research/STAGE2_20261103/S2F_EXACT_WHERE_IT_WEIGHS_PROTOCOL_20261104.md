## Question

Stage 1 (b)'s ternary experts cost −8.52 points. In each token, read the slot with the highest routing weight from the
original Q4_K_M and the others ternary. How much of −8.52 returns, and for how many milliseconds?

## Thesis line (rule 4)

On Ling-flash, one exact slot in every MoE layer costs +3.92 ms on the desk (29.45 ms stored against 13.77 ms ternary at
k = 4, over 4 slots). The room under 24.7 tok/s is 1.12 ms with test 2b's cell 1 (28% of the layers) and 2.29 ms with cell 2
(58%). All layers exact gives 23.1 or 23.7 tok/s: under the line. So the arm that decides is the one that fits the room.

- **Late layers carry it,** the reverse of DyMoE's reading on one layer alone.

  falls to 0.98 when it happens in early layers: rounding flips the routing. Top-1 here is not read below 0.02.
- **Test 3 left no guilty experts,** so the arm is the highest weight.
