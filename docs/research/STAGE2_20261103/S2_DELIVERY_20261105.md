- **Specific:** the numbers; the fused QKV with q/k norms and RoPE on half of each head; one dense layer; a shared expert beside 256 routed experts; sigmoid routing with groups; 32 heads on 4; the vocabulary of 157,184; a and b of the tail; which layers carry the return.

## 8. Recommendation

**Do not open stage 3 yet. Part 2's first test goes on the organs: st_both again, same training and budget, in the four-level grid that suits them (asym: {−1, 0, 1, 2} with a sign per row, which keeps a zero, and which step 1 ranked first on both), judged on top-1 and panels against −13.36.** About 5.5 h of GPU and 3 h of panels, on mini. Experts now have two ways back and part 2 trains them in bal; the organs have none, and they weigh 20 ms. On the experts the grid moved 7 points at equal bytes, more than any exact slot per ms, and it is by far the cheapest thing left to try before buying 11 times the tokens (about 26 h on layers 0-5, with 4 downstream blocks).

**What would disprove it:** asym st_both within noise of −13.36. Then the grid is not the organs' lever, and part 2 buys tokens and downstream blocks. If those fail too, the budget's only configuration stays near 15-16 tok/s, and the decision moves to the speed line.
