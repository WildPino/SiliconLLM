rollout and C-engine numerical-parity rung 1. Earlier source-binding,

## STRAT-01 document rollout

The frozen paired 96-document rollout is `PASS_DOCUMENT_ROLLOUT`. Q4 produced
two newly degenerate documents, both `loop8x3` in code; BF16 had no code or
prose degeneration and one `technical_general` degeneration. The registered
failure threshold is at least three Q4-only degeneracies, so the gate passes
by one. Both arms generated 256 tokens per document on average and had zero
EOS terminations, `run32`, or `empty` events. Exact generated sequences match
on 1/96 documents; the mean common prefix is 15.104166666666666 tokens, a
diagnostic only.

See the [canonical result](../donor_adaptation/probes/STRAT_01_GIGACHAT31_DOCUMENT_ROLLOUT_RESULT_20260921.md)
and raw adjudication at
`benchmarks/donor_adaptation/density/results/strat01_gigachat_rollout_v1/rollout_adjudication.json`.
The measured runtime totals are reference-runtime quality-run durations, not
accepted-token throughput; they do not belong in `SPEED_LEDGER.md`.

## STRAT-01 C-engine parity rung 0

The accepted Q4 artifact now passes [engine rung 0](../donor_adaptation/probes/STRAT_01_GIGACHAT31_ENGINE_RUNG0_RESULT_20260921.md).
At committed engine revision `10a44b5`, the early-dispatched native C path
verifies the exact 6,474,702,976-byte file and SHA-256, parses GGUF v3 in
bounded memory, and emits all 414 tensor descriptors. The descriptor digest
is exactly equal to the pinned llama.cpp reader, and all 414 names/shapes
match the BF16 source-binding report. The final apparatus also passes its
malformed/substitution controls and the historical engine kernel self-test.

Rung 0 itself establishes identity/layout only. Its 25.96-second full-file
hash is not a load-time or rate measurement and is excluded from
`SPEED_LEDGER.md`. Rung 1 below subsequently establishes dequantization,
stored-row orientation, and scalar matvec parity for its three named tensors;
coverage of other tensors and quantized layouts, DeepSeek2 operators,
tokenizer semantics, logits, greedy decoding, C-path quality, RAM, and the
>=50 tok/s lower-CI target remain open.

The [engine compatibility audit](../donor_adaptation/audits/STRAT_01_GIGACHAT31_ENGINE_COMPATIBILITY_AUDIT_20260921.md)
now identifies the tokenizer, operator, layout, and container mismatches. It
also establishes that the Qwen-oriented `donor_engine.c` is only a source of
implementation patterns, not a semantically compatible execution path.

Rung 0 is closed and must not be repeated. Its next step was a separately
frozen numerical-parity rung 1; the result below now records that cell and its
exact no-duplication boundary. Neither identity/layout nor rung-1 scalar
parity supports a model-quality or rate claim.

## STRAT-01 C-engine numerical parity rung 1

The accepted Q4 artifact passes
[engine rung 1](../donor_adaptation/probes/STRAT_01_GIGACHAT31_ENGINE_RUNG1_RESULT_20260921.md).
The preregistered brief is commit `c802954`; implementation/run is
`d2f6351`. At the exact 6,474,702,976-byte artifact SHA-256
`68a8732fb5cee04f83ebffd7924e15c534d4442c5a43d2ba9e2041fe310b8deb`, C and
pinned `gguf-py` have identical complete canonical float32 dequant digests
for Q4_K `blk.0.attn_q.weight`, Q5_0 `blk.0.attn_k_b.weight`, and Q6_K
`blk.0.ffn_down.weight`. Every stored row was compared; maximum normalized
residuals are respectively `1.5472069223217056e-07`, `0`, and
`1.530169167198841e-07`, below the frozen `2e-6` limit. The combined result
is `PASS_ENGINE_RUNG1`.

This proves numerical decoding and scalar stored-row matvec parity only for
these three tensors. Their codec/scalar-row cells must never be repeated on
the same artifact, inputs, and estimand. Rung 1 does not establish optimized
kernel speed, attention/MLA/YaRN, dense FFN or MoE semantics, tokenizer, full
logits/generation, C-path quality, RAM, or accepted-token throughput. Its
compile/C/reference timings are operational diagnostics and are excluded
from `SPEED_LEDGER.md`.

The next coordinate is a separately frozen rung 2 for organ/layer semantic
parity. A likely staged sequence is a narrow block-0 attention cell, block-0
dense SwiGLU, and one routed/shared MoE layer, each with fixed source token IDs,
positions, and reference intermediates. Exact subcells and gates remain to be
preregistered; no rung-2 implementation exists. HumanEval remains
`PENDING_SANDBOX`; tokenizer, full-base logits/greedy generation/C quality,
RAM, and the >=50 tok/s lower-CI rate gate remain open.
