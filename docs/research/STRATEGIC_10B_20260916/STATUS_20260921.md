## STRAT-01 document rollout

The frozen paired 96-document rollout is `PASS_DOCUMENT_ROLLOUT`. Q4 produced
two newly degenerate documents, both `loop8x3` in code; BF16 had no code or
prose degeneration and one `technical_general` degeneration. The registered
failure threshold is at least three Q4-only degeneracies, so the gate passes
by one. Both arms generated 256 tokens per document on average and had zero
EOS terminations, `run32`, or `empty` events. Exact generated sequences match
on 1/96 documents; the mean common prefix is 15.104166666666666 tokens, a
diagnostic only.

See the [canonical result](../donor_adaptation/probes/STRAT_01_GIGACHAT31_DOCUMENT_ROLLOUT_RESULT_20260921.md)
and raw adjudication at
`benchmarks/donor_adaptation/density/results/strat01_gigachat_rollout_v1/rollout_adjudication.json`.
The measured runtime totals are reference-runtime quality-run durations, not
accepted-token throughput; they do not belong in `SPEED_LEDGER.md`.
