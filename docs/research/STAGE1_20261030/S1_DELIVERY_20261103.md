## What the stage says

The binding constraint is no longer speed: it is the quality of a whole model at about 2 bits.
- Speed: the existing kernels and k = 4 give 25.6 on the desk, if every organ is ternary.
- Quality: of the five organs, only the routed experts have been tested at ternary. With the block-wise training they
  alone cost −8.52, above the 5-point budget the whole model shares. One layer alone is free; nineteen stacked are not,
  and sequential block-wise training recovered 17% of the post-hoc KL.
- Attention, the shared expert, the dense FFN and the head have not been tested at ternary at all. Together they are
  57% of the bytes per token at k = 4 as stored (Q4_K_M).

## Recommendation for stage 2

**Before assembling the recipe, answer one question on Ling-mini: can the model with every weight-bearing organ
ternary, at decode-only k = 4, come inside the 5-point budget with a training that sees the stacked model (end-to-end
distillation on the original's logits) at an affordable cost?**

- First the literature on converting a pretrained model to ternary with distillation, read in the papers (rule 2):
  how many training tokens the recoveries needed, and how they fail. The cell is sized from that, against the training
  budget, before the protocol.
- A first mechanism test on the experts already ternary from (b) (their weights are the starting point): does a
  training that sees the stack move −8.52 a long way toward the budget?
- Over 10 GPU-h, so it goes to an independent reviewer before it is registered (rule 6).
- No engine work and no kernel of ours until it answers. The kernel gap (about 0.6 tok/s) is smaller than any quality
  answer is worth.

**What would disprove it:**
- the literature shows that recovering a pretrained model to ternary needs far more training tokens than the budget
  allows; or
- the mechanism test with a training that sees the stack leaves the experts at least as far from the budget as the
  block-wise training did (Δ still under −5 at its centre).

Either outcome would mean the 24.7 line is not reachable on this machine by the per-weight route at an affordable
cost. The next decision is then about the line or the route, not about a kernel.
