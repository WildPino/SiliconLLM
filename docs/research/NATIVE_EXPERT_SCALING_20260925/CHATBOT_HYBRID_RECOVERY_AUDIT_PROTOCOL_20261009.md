# Saved-only balanced recovery audit

9 October2026. PRE-OBSERVATION auditor contract, no source/student calls.
Auditor is not launched before the exact original recovery worker is terminal.
It accepts a complete primary or retained failed/partial state and preserves
that distinction. A successful audit does not promote a partial pilot.

## Reused inputs and uncertainty

Reuse exact original binding/order,terminal or launcher-failure receipt,actual
completed before/after F32 logits/case JSON/update JSON,teacher BF16 packets,
and actual final or complete-boundary recovery checkpoint. Verify original
input extents/hashes even on a failure where launcher's success-only postcheck
was not reached. The original launcher's exact bytes are archived before its
subsequent schema extension and checked against the original bound SHA; the
new current launcher is bound separately. No model/generation/training/native replay or missing output
reconstruction. Code/current runtime files/Python and all consumed data bound
before audit. Full original DLL/library trees are not independently certified.

Question:do saved full-vocabulary F64 metrics/IDs support the reported GPU
reductions/eligibility decision, and does actual model/Adam/RNG state match the
completed update boundary? If disagreement occurs, retain it and prevent
promotion. Empty/missing after outputs remain missing. Orphan raw packets or
checkpoint/durable-log discrepancies fail the audit and remain repair evidence.
