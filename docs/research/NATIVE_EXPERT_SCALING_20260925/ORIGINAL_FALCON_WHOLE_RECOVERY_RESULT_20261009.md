## Consequence for the converter

The [readout diagnostic](ORIGINAL_READOUT_INFORMATION_RESULT_20261009.md) showed
arbitrary features can express much lower sampled loss; they are not functions of
history. This actual dose learns partial probability recovery but leaves that
history-function gap open. Fresh core, source24->target6 composition, projection,
gate approximation, ternarization and routing are confounded, not individually
convicted by this experiment. No universal D256 limit or useless-bank theorem.

[Current next](ORIGINAL_ENGINE_TRANSFER_NEXT_20261009.md) selects principled
source history/state transport and checks operator/composition omissions before
new training. Preserve original LUT/ternary sparse execution; price compact core
changes against complete endpoint cost. Original tiny-model speed cannot be
inherited by this full-vocabulary artifact. Useful same-artifact chatbot+50,
useful RAM-driven n/CPU routing mass/DRAM and additional families remain required.
