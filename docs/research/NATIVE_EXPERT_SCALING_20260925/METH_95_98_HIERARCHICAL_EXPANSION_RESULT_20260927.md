# METH-95–98: exact 10× expansion, child learning, fresh development

## Bound artifacts and method

METH-95 starts from the METH-56 update-512 BF16-donor E128 product-key checkpoint (`8371262a1461a44fcedf12d129059d8b8ab1fd9860e032df8661a30eff187072`) on the pinned Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct source (`fdf756fa7fcbe7404d5c60e26bff1a0c8b8aa1f72ced49e7dd0210fe288fb7fe`). Each learned parent gets ten children with copied rank-8 A/B factors. The rank-64 parent router and its top-four softmax remain unchanged; a fixed seeded rank-32 child router selects one child per chosen parent. METH-96 trains child B factors only for 128 raw/chat updates, preserving parent keys, child keys, A factors and the donor. METH-97 freezes 24 source-disjoint documents and prompts before METH-98 scores them. See the [expansion protocol](METH_95_HIERARCHICAL_EXPANSION_PROTOCOL_20260927.md) and [training protocol](METH_96_CHILD_SPECIALIZATION_PROTOCOL_20260927.md).

## Measured result

The [METH-95 parity record](meth95_hierarchical_e1280_parity_result.json) shows bitwise-identical final logits on a 128-token raw sequence and a 173-token chat prompt. Greedy tokens, parent routes and parent scores are identical. Four child experts remain selected per position. Across those 301 positions, each layer visits 284–437 of its 1,280 child slots. FP32 resident child A/B factors total 1,761,607,680 bytes; parent keys 5,652,480, child projections 2,752,512 and child keys 3,932,160. The ideal selected BF16 factor payload is 2,752,512 bytes per token across 24 layers. These are byte counts, not measured CPU traffic or throughput.

The [METH-96 training record](meth96_hierarchical_child_specialization_result.json) binds the 128-update child checkpoint (`726c47612d3ccd60dc3f8f9a696a98e5c9d0619c968e7c5e6704afae06c1a836`). Per-layer changed child slots versus the copied parent range from 1,115 to 1,263; the same range of slots received a selected route during training. A posthoc sibling comparison finds 1,061–1,152 slots per layer with at least one B coordinate >0.001 away from child zero. The training peak was 7.62 GB GPU allocation and 4.60 GB process RSS; runtime 168 seconds. This establishes parameter distinction and exposure, not additional useful capacity.

On the [METH-97 fresh manifest](meth97_hierarchical_dev_manifest.json), [METH-98](meth98_hierarchical_development_result.json) records pooled BPB 1.208005 for BF16+E128 and 1.206712 for BF16+E1280, an E1280 gain of 0.001293. All three category BPB deltas improve. Pooled donor prompt top-1 is 94.7702% for E128 and 93.6609% for E1280, a loss of 1.10935 points. The frozen pooled noninferiority gate permits only one point; it fails. The three category top-1 deltas pass their two-point gates. METH-98 decision: **stop hierarchical development for this checkpoint**. The result is close to the gate, but the threshold remains unchanged.

METH-99 tests a frozen teacher-decision retention continuation from the METH-96 checkpoint on a new METH-100 manifest. It must not reuse the viewed METH-97 result as its quality gate. The full CPU child router/LUT, cold DRAM access, integrated `engine.c` model and ≥50 accepted tok/s remain unmeasured. The compact-core blind-semantic failure from METH-90 is also independent and unresolved.
