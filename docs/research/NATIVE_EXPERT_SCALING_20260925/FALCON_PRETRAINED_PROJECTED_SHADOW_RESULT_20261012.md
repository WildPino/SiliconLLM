## Decision: encode the changing source state, then train

Close this FIXED-global-P projected pretrained path as a useful warm artifact
under the inherited quality screen. It remains a mechanically valid transfer
control and reusable exporter/trajectory dataset. More dose or rare route-set
corrections are not justified as sufficient repairs by these observations.

Separate next problems:

1. **Angular coordinates:** existing single P captures only~12%-14% intermediate
   source energy. Test FIT-only layer-specific source residual bases/dual read-write
   metrics or conditional affine charts on retained data; not a repeat of the
   failed384-channel recurrence pruning objective. Energy is only a proxy and
   must subsequently be tested in complete behavioral composition.
2. **Radial information:** true source h=Pz+r has norm including discarded r.
   Correct restriction norm assumes r=0; that exact algebra does not recover the
   omitted full-source radius or directions. Price/predict that additional norm
   information, or encode affine/conditional reconstruction before claiming a
   source-function approximation. Do not silently change the completed model.
3. **Residual transport:** with layer-specific decode P_l/read Q_l, skip becomes
   Q_(l+1)^T P_l, not identity. Include that operator and exact norm metric/gamma
   fold in the converter and its actual native cost. Affine charts also require
   explicit centers/bias/constant-coordinate treatment, not free redundancy.
4. **Cheap conditional endpoint:** source large feature/state/full FFN/attention
   costs still need compact conditional functions, real packed LUT/ternary
   execution, structured IDs+masses and physical DRAM. A floating donor-derived
   shadow or spectral-energy pass cannot replace useful same-artifact50.

experiment using existing source boundaries, source weights and the new target
trajectories; predeclare proxy/gates and then actual composed validation. No new
