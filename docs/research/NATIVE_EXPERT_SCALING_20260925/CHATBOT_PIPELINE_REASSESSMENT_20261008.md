## Algebraic problems kept separate

1. **Representation:** original SwiGLU f(x)=sum_j d_j SiLU(g_j x)(u_j x).
   Target shared g plus sum_selected mass_e h_e must represent the omitted
   function at a convenient active cost. More stored experts alone do not fix
   a per-cell missing output/curvature subspace. Redundant donor atoms across
   cells are allowed; their useful coverage and active cost need measurement.
2. **Transfer/coverage:** fitting many finite responses or exact16 jets does
   not determine the function on unseen states. Qualified previous objectives
   fail their outside-FIT necessary fidelity screen. This is evidence against
   those constructions, not a universal lower bound on conditional conversion.
3. **Composition:** local RMS is a filter, not a guarantee of logits/tasks.
   Full layers/attention/norm/head/cache/rounding and realized own-history must
   compose and be tested on excluded data; teacher history cannot substitute.
4. **Economics:** price the complete model, not one expert. The current SINGLE
   rank16 proposal is295104512MAC/592186464 logical coefficientB/token and
   1080411488 stored payload for ALL24. At50 IDs/s its logical-byte product is
   29.6093232GB/s; this is dimensional accounting, NOT a physical DRAM lower
   bound or a hardware throughput measurement. Cache/reuse/context/backend
   must be measured on an actual eligible complete artifact.
5. **Selection/scaling:** as n grows, correct winner alone is insufficient;
   normalized selected mass changes the represented function. RAM permits
   storage, but does not prove route resolution/capacity/useful throughput.
