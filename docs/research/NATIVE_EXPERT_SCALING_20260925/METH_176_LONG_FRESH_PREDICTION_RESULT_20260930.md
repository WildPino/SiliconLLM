# METH-176: long-trained E12,800 fresh prediction gate fails bootstrap

**Decision: stop before generation, PIQA and blind grounding.** The matched E12,800 candidate improves pooled BPB against the long-trained E1,280 control by 0.00011440 on 24 untouched, source-disjoint documents. This clears the frozen point-estimate requirement of at least 0.00005 BPB. The paired, category-stratified 10,000-draw source bootstrap has 5th-percentile control-minus-candidate gain **-0.00004190**, below the strictly positive requirement. Thus the prespecified first quality gate fails. A favorable pooled point estimate is insufficient to claim useful additional experts.

The [manifest](meth176_long_external_manifest.json), SHA-256 `9ac93c3b6e7eeaf67e928b3973f7042ad6ad002f2f5eed7f810046f8242d9029`, was committed at `a7353ed` before inference. It binds eight code files, eight PG19 shard-9 books and eight technical documents, plus exact 4,095-byte spans, prompt IDs and provenance exclusions. The [result](meth176_long_prediction_result.json) has SHA-256 `46ad97c5810e18bb98a0284d631dfce193eea9874b0cb8b9dde1137b0d0b498d`.

These are paired comparison metrics; the donor's self-agreement is 100% by definition.

The bootstrap used the frozen seed `176176` with eight source-level draws within each category per replicate. An independent calculation from the saved per-source nats and byte counts reproduced the exact 5th percentile. Its median is +0.00011532 and 95th percentile +0.00027258 BPB; 14/24 individual sources favor the candidate. These descriptive values do not alter the failed frozen gate. All four arms used identical frozen document and prompt IDs. Eight-prompt pretraining BF16 parity was exact, and the continued control and candidate banks passed exact BF16 readback with their METH-175 SHA-256 bindings. The candidate's document route used the true previous token and absolute position for each scored window.

The local RTX 3060 run took 180.89 seconds, with 4,673,313,792 bytes peak allocated GPU memory and 14,461,054,976 bytes RSS at result write, below the frozen caps. No T4 was used. Shard 9 and these 24 sources are consumed by this single adjudication; do not tune a follow-up candidate on them. The stronger training duration moved the point estimate from METH-156's adverse result on different sources to a small favorable result here, but those two cohorts cannot establish a paired learning-duration effect. The open work is to find and prospectively test a stronger specialist-learning signal or architecture on new training and untouched evaluation sources. CPU LUT/full-model throughput and 10B/100B transfer remain unproved.
