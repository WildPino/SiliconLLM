# METH-181: out-of-calibration diagonal activation-weighted 10B expert rank

**Uncertainty and evidence.** METH-180 rejects direct unweighted rank-192 factors on 27 bound GigaChat BF16 expert matrices: median optimal weight energy is 47.243%, far below the frozen 95% screen. Weight norm may overvalue directions rarely used by the donor. METH-05/06 already collected source-ID BF16 input-squared sums and per-expert counts on disjoint English/code/technical (106 chunks) and Cyrillic (125 chunks) calibration corpora. This experiment asks whether a *diagonal input-covariance proxy* changes that rank-192 decision on a distinct input domain. It does not measure true output error under full covariance, nonlinear composition, BPB, or native throughput.

**Frozen inputs.** Use the exact METH-180 3 layers (1, 13, 25), 3 experts (0, 32, 63) and gate/up/down projections: 27 BF16 matrices, excluding MTP. Bind source revision `189fff27a1dee68473960c3d5bca53e0e07a3191`, METH-180's source index/config/shard/tensor hashes, and the two preexisting imatrix files by SHA-256: English/code/technical 106-chunk `ef5de87fc4fa0d1382ed2988e59e9472fb1d7fb2c9bed0e8edcced6ff9bd4f9c` and Cyrillic 125-chunk `5c529f9009f0f241dfc50d0a1f598b0e183c137693a951f04963dc729a344d08`. Do not use their merged matrix to fit. GGUF imatrix `blk.L.ffn_{gate,up,down}_exps.weight.in_sum2` contains one input second-moment vector per expert; `.counts` gives its exposure. Require at least 64 observations for each of the 27 instances in **both** files, matching METH-05's exposure floor, or stop before SVD. No new calibration or heldout examples are selected.

**Frozen estimand and comparison.** For each `W` (outputs × inputs), form `v_train = in_sum2_train/count_train` and `v_test = in_sum2_test/count_test`. Validate finite strictly positive vectors of exact input width. Let `D = diag(sqrt(v_train))`. The truncated SVD of `W D` is the optimal rank-192 reconstruction under the *training diagonal* expected squared output error; recover its approximation to `W` by multiplying right factors by `D^-1`. On the disjoint test diagonal, report `1 - ||(W-W_hat) diag(sqrt(v_test))||_F² / ||W diag(sqrt(v_test))||_F²`. As a fixed comparator, compute the ordinary rank-192 SVD of `W` and evaluate its approximation on the same test diagonal. Also report the within-domain weighted optimum and the train/test second-moment ratios, as diagnostics. Use FP32 SVD with FP64 error accumulation; verify direct reconstruction and SVD energy on training values. All 27 matrix identities, sample counts, residuals and scores go into one small JSON; do not write factor weights.

**Decision.** The diagonal activation-aware rank-192 path is eligible for a separately frozen full-model quality and selected-kernel/cost test only if the **disjoint Cyrillic test** has median energy at least 95%, every sampled matrix at least 90%, and no matrix underperforms the ordinary-SVD comparator by more than 0.5 percentage point. Otherwise reject this diagonal-weighted direct rank-192 path. A pass is only a proxy screen, not proof of donor-relative quality, 50 accepted tok/s, or 100B transfer. Do not revise the rank, sample or threshold after seeing scores.

**Budget and stop.** CPU only, six BLAS threads, no GPU/T4; stop at 15 minutes, above 6 GiB RSS, on a missing/mismatched source or imatrix SHA/shape/count, nonfinite moments, or numerical check failure. Preserve any apparatus failure separately. Existing donor-adaptation C parity work remains paused; do not rerun its producer or graph.
