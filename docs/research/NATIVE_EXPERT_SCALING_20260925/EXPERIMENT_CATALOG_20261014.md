14 October 2026. Goal **INCOMPLETE**. This catalog separates numerical/code-path qualification, local operator fidelity, whole-model quality, same-artifact accepted CPU speed, and useful conditional capacity. A PASS in one category never implies a PASS in another. Values belong only to the named artifact and measurement scope.

## Classification

**NUM-PASS** means independent arithmetic/equation audit passed. **LOCAL-PASS/FAIL** means scoped operator fidelity. **WHOLE-PASS/FAIL** means whole-model quality on the specified corpus. **RATE-PASS/FAIL** means accepted useful CPU generation rate for that same artifact. **CAPACITY-PASS/FAIL** means distinct useful conditional functions under a charged route. **DIAGNOSTIC/PENDING/UNREVIEWED** marks bounded or insufficiently reviewed evidence. None subsumes another.

## Evidence boundary

No reviewed artifact simultaneously has donor-relative chatbot quality, a compact reusable SSM/SWA, useful conditional capacity selected by a charged CPU route emitting IDs and masses, measured DRAM traffic, and ≥50 accepted batch-one CPU tok/s. Historical Switch rate and Falcon full-width quality are different artifacts. Current Falcon recurrent/generator costs exceed the desired budget in tested compositions. No reviewed evidence establishes multi-family 10B/100B scaling or sustainable conversion cost.
