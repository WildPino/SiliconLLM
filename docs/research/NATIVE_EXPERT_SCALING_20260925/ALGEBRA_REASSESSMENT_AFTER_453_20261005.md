## 2. Why this geometry differs from a fixed low-rank factor

At a query, only columns J={j:c_j!=0} of WO contribute. Its effective output lives
in the span of those columns, whose rank is at most min(D,|J|). Mean |J|222.4167
at D768/M3072 does NOT imply a global rank222 representation: J depends on input
and expert, its observed union can exceed D, and stored matrices remain complete.
Original-coordinate ReLU supplies this conditional coordinate geometry. A generic
Hadamard transform of its activation would mix zeros into dense coordinates.

output bases;451's BOTH-I4 recipe failed margins. Those outcomes do not rule out
dynamic coordinate sparsity or every conditional information metric. Conversely,

General RAM feasibility has the form:

    RAM >= M_core + L*E*b_candidate + M_router(E) + M_context + M_work + metadata

    E_100/E_10 = (P_100-P_fixed)/(P_10-P_fixed)

The user's approximately10x expectation follows only when these geometries are

count, top-k or source families need their actual tensors; parameter labels alone
do not determine E or active cost. This algebra is not an actual100B qualification.

## 4. Why a smaller footprint does not yet prove a faster LUT

A WI adjacent-activation pair table has(D/2)*256 I32 entries,512*D bytes:
393,216B at D768. It must be built for each new query. Each of D*M/2 packed bytes
then selects one I32 table entry. At this shape1,179,648 lookups request4,718,592B
of table values, in addition to packed weights/scales and table construction.
These requests may hit caches; they are not an extra4.72MB of measured DRAM.
Counting only unique table bytes would also omit the repeated access cost.

Direct WI has D*M=2,359,296 signed products plus decode/scaling. Pair lookup
trades products for builds, dependent indexed reads and adds. Sparse WO's mean
D*|J|=170,816 products versus2,359,296 dense products is a genuine operation-count
reduction, but scans/indices/layout/accumulation and the rest of the model remain.
Neither instruction count nor unique byte count predicts the winner reliably.

Thus NEW454 needs the unchanged qualified original C FFN primitive as baseline,
both prospective direct/LUT candidates, exact arithmetic first and measured cost
afterwards. An artificially slow new source baseline would make the conclusion
unusable. Preserve compiler/runtime and original routines; charge one-worker
OpenMP/setup conventions explicitly and avoid extrapolating to391's full3workers.
