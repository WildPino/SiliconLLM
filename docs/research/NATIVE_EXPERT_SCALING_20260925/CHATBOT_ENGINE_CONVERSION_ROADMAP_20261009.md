## Fixed destination, variables of the conversion

The final expert matrices execute through packed ternary LUT primitives. A
compact recurrent/bounded-attention core and F32 sensitive organs are retained.
The conversion may use higher precision/wider intermediate models and prolonged
training offline. Any operator extension needs an explicit cost and a quality
reason. A donor-shaped intermediate is conversion scaffolding, not the final
performance result. Neither literal original D256/L6/V1024 dimensions nor an
arbitrary generic donor runtime defines completion.

For n banks/site, fixed selected k, width h, core width D and L sites:

\[
P_{banks}=3LDhn,\qquad P_{active}=3LDhk,
\qquad t=t_{core}+t_{state}+t_{route}(n)+t_{active}+t_{head}+t_{chat}.
\]

Increasing useful n is the storage/capacity dial. Widening D/h, increasing L/k,
or retaining full donor projections is an increase in active work. Current
selected expert work is4 times original D256/L6/k8/h128 work because D and L
both doubled. Its V65537/D512 head has about128 times the original V1024/D256
products. The new46-59 raw one-core decode measurements include these costs.
The original701.7/s and modeled large-pool rates retain their measured/modeled
scopes. They are a motivation, not a chatbot conversion proof.

A dense SwiGLU can be partitioned exactly as a sum of row groups. Executing a
normalized selected mixture is a different operation. For overlapping experts
\(g_e=\sum_j c_{ej}f_j\), exact coverage requires
\(\sum_{e\in S}\alpha_e c_{ej}=1\) for the needed atoms, or another valid
representation of their correlated responses. Adding copies alone does not
ensure this. The common/private variant is a testable way to share recurring
information; its frozen zero-output initialization has not been executed and
does not solve transfer by itself.

Operator errors also propagate through the changed trajectory. Under valid
local Lipschitz bounds, \(\|e_{l+1}\|\le L_l\|e_l\|+\|\delta_l\|\).
The bound is conditional, not a measured quality certificate. Separate local
passes cannot be added into a whole-model KL guarantee. Whole outputs and
generated histories remain the final observations.
