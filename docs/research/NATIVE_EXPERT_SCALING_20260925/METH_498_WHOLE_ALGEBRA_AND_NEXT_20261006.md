## 3. The current source prior is for a different function

For the ideal bias-free two-matrix ReLU donor, with dequantized weights:

    F_e(x)=L0_e x+E_e(x)
    L0_e=.5 V_e W_e; E_e(x)=.5 V_e abs(W_e x).

494's C0 is an UNWEIGHTED Gaussian projection of E_e onto shared absolute
features. The current recipe instead initializes a_e C0 and fixes a_e L0,
then asks B/bias to learn p_e(x)F_e(x). Here a_e is only mean development mass.
The Gaussian distribution and constant amplitude are explicit approximations.

Let p+=(p(x)+p(-x))/2 and p-=(p(x)-p(-x))/2. Then, over real arithmetic:

    odd(pF)=p+ L0 x+p- E(x)
    even(pF)=p- L0 x+p+ E(x).

A fixed a_e L0 plus globally even features has odd part a_e L0 x. This explains
a structural mismatch in the prior and target; it does NOT prove that mismatch
accounts for9.52%, or impose a measured lower bound on winner-selected cells.
Those cells need not contain both x and -x. Actual quantized inputs and F32
programs also require their own arithmetic checks.

Changing to explicit p(x) multiplication restores the donor decomposition as
a relevant prior for the UNWEIGHTED function. It changes the hypothesis; it is
not an exact restoration of the donor's rounded program, nor a quality theorem.

For preserved old logits z and added logits z_new:

    p_old(e)=exp(z_e)/Z_old
    p_new(e)=p_old(e)/(1+Z_added/Z_old).
