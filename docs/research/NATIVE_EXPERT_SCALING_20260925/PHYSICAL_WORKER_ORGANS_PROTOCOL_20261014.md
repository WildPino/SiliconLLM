# Dominant preserved organs: physical-worker cost screen

14 October 2026. Prospective CPU-only inquiry before unexecuted depth6/12 arms.
The admitted generator and recurrence timings used one physical worker. A linear
storage/depth extrapolation is not a universal multicore lower bound. This screen
changes ONLY row scheduling of the23 contextual generators and24 integer
readouts, retaining actual BF weights, eight AVX FMA lanes/reduction order,
BF rounding/MuP, original128-column I16 reduction, scales and BF output packing.
It reuses the qualified physical-core binding implementation, verifies topology
and per-thread affinity, and pins the OpenMP runtime/compiler and actual inputs.

## Fixed design

- Arms1/3/6 actual physical workers. One persistent six-worker OpenMP runtime;
  dynamic teams disabled. One-worker arm calls the unchanged serial functions.
  OMP_WAIT_POLICY=PASSIVE/KMP_BLOCKTIME=0 avoids idle workers spinning during
  the serial arm; wake/barrier costs remain in every measured parallel call.
  Static row partitioning, unchanged dot products, no cross-worker reduction.
- Same four FIT/DEV short/long histories and final16 positions as the qualified
  generator/recurrence cost screens. No new quality sample, source forward,
  fitting, GPU, RESERVED or reduced-model arm. Stored actual own-history h and
  normalized readout inputs/scales are supplied, not generated by this program.
- Resident and parameter-evicted modes. Every arm receives its own eviction of
  all23 BF matrices/MuP and all24 readout q/scale arrays. Flush is retained outside
  timed calls. Warmup excluded by fixed policy, all measured tails retained.
- Rotate all six permutations of1/3/6 at each position; measure24 readouts and
  23 generators in layer order (readout0, then generator/readout at sites1..23).
  Retain full-group and separate operator timings and all raw outputs. No overhead
  subtraction, best-run selection or new shortlist/rank/precision threshold.
- Compile O3/AVX2/FMA/znver2, FP contraction off except explicit intrinsics,
  OpenMP. Same binary for all arms. FP control0x1f80 on master and workers.
- Full producer byte comparison between arms and against qualified stored
  readout arrays. Separate stored auditor replays every consumed original serial
  generator/readout call, checks every candidate raw/GG/field/integer/F64/BF
  cell, sample coverage/order/accounting, descriptors and summary arithmetic.
  Scheduling equivalence is tested; the original independent arithmetic
  qualification is inherited for these same operands, not rerun under a new name.

Eligibility: all exact numeric checks, topology, full coverage and input/output
seal pass; descriptive joint cost<=20ms is an organ-only feasibility screen.
It cannot certify useful50: convolution, recurrent update, normalization/input
quantization, FFN, attention, routing, head and frontend remain outside timing.
The supplied internal operands do not constitute a joined decoder. Even a passed
organ budget leaves the remaining actual operators to price and integrate.
Report all three arms irrespective of outcome; do not promote only the fastest.

One pipeline including compilation/binding, production, full stored audit and
closure: <=3600s CPU wall, <=4GiB OS peak, <=2GiB new family output, <=6 physical
workers, no GPU. Pin actual consumed source/trace/reference/parameter/runtime
files and seal again after the complete pipeline. Preserve first failure; no
automatic retry or replay of fitting. Launch and pause on long wait, user resumes.
Use a fresh named family; no writes to parent data or unrelated dirty files.
