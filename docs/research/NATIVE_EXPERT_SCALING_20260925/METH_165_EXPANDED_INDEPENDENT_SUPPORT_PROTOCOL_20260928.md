# METH-165: independent support expansion for the tenfold expert ladder

**Trigger and uncertainty.** Execute teacher acquisition only if the fixed 138-tuple METH-162 route passes METH-164's separate PG19 source/context-length gates. METH-159's 2,560 independent pairs give minimum active content-row median 39 versus >=50 even after the METH-162 hot-slot repair. Under roughly proportional exposure, 3,840 distinct pairs may raise the weakest median above 50; this is a planning estimate, not a result. The decision is whether the 10× E1,280→E12,800 ladder has enough training selections per specialist to justify matched long-budget learning. Do not reduce expert count to pass.

**Frozen inputs.** Use the exact METH-158 H0 training-ID matrix and tokenizer hashes. Deterministically select 1,280 new chat rows and 1,280 new raw rows with seed `meth165-independent-train-165165`, excluding all 5,120 METH-158 selected rows, earlier METH-43/136 training rows, prior dev/evaluation rows and prior external-audit excerpt fragments. Use the same first-96-token METH-43 chat prompt template and deterministic 128-token raw windows at offsets 96–384, with disjoint source rows. Freeze row IDs, offsets, source and prompt/window hashes, old manifest and exclusion hashes before donor generation. No PG19 shard-9 content may be read.

Generate one new greedy continuation of at most 128 tokens for each of the 1,280 frozen prompts from the same BF16 Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct donor revision, cached on the local RTX 3060. Store small independently hashed shards for resumption; require exact prompt/response readback, EOS/repetition counts, and a verified merged artifact. Do not repeat METH-158 responses or train on the METH-164 PG19 validation prompts.

**Prospective route-support screen.** Forward the frozen E1,280 source model on every new raw/chat pair, recover its actual four source children in all 24 layers and apply the frozen METH-162 table with the unchanged nine-way content hash. Require BF16 teacher/control parity and all hashes. Combine the new per-layer 12,800-slot all/content histograms with the saved METH-162 replay of the original 2,560 pairs, summing corresponding slots exactly; check grandchild-to-child identity and four selections/token. On the combined 3,840 pairs require, in every layer, at least 10,000 occupied content slots and median active content-row selections >=50; require across layers at most 50% of content rows under 32 selections, global nine-way maximum-load ratio <=1.25, and worst hot-parent grandchild share <=25%, using the METH-159 definitions. Also report each new-only cell and every layer; a combined pass cannot hide a new-only concentration. The old 2,560-pair METH-159 failure remains recorded.

**Cost and stop.** Manifest cap 5 minutes/8 GiB RSS. Teacher acquisition budget 70 minutes of GPU work, each shard <=15 minutes, <=10.5 GiB allocated GPU memory and <=20 GiB RSS. Route-screen budget 40 minutes/10.5 GiB GPU/20 GiB RSS. No T4. Stop on any bound-source, parity, count or resource failure. A support pass licenses freezing a matched E1,280 control/E12,800 shared-base-plus-residual training design with equal 3,840-update objective and budget, plus a new untouched shard-9 quality audit. It alone does not prove useful experts, donor quality, compact LUT cost or native >=50 accepted tok/s.
