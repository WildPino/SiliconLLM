# Primary-report readback after the experiment inventory

14 October 2026. Goal ACTIVE/INCOMPLETE. This is an evidence review, not a new
experiment, model execution, changed gate or artifact promotion. The
[catalog](EXPERIMENT_CATALOG_20261014.md) indexes609 result-report files in this

## Directly read primary results

- METH177/178/179: large pools can have bounded selected-kernel cost, but the
  tested E128000/E12800 relative gate failed1.3093x>1.25. Trained children had
  only2.318% route-weighted within-parent B variation; rotating child IDs did
  not robustly worsen BPB. New bytes and changed weights do not demonstrate
  useful conditional capacity. Repeating that hash-route training is unsupported.
- METH185: exact-activation norm-ranked top2048/4864 source Qwen channels still
  lose16.49% median/20.85% p95 local output. This is not an optimal-subset lower
  bound, and says nothing universal about trained partitions. It blocks treating
  direct neuron omission as an already-qualified sparse FFN. Compare the newer
  Falcon remainder oracle, which subtracts a shared response and has different
  donor, metric and retained-width obligations.
- METH222/225/230: input-function alignment, fast C fixtures and low FIT error
  individually occurred without quality/useful-n admission. E160 affine maps
  worsen validation; nonlinear common fails the intermediate accuracy gate;
  one source-anchored child has one unique fit input and no slope information.
  Future conditional banks need sufficient distinct exposure and functional
  ablation, not a parameter-hash difference test alone.
- METH364/363/359: M364 is RATE FAIL45.111 ordinary accepted IDs/s, prose24.698;
  its single-core source256 quality pass remains scoped. M376 separately passes
  ordinary63.525 but prose34.779 with six physical workers. M391 on independently
  trained original128/three workers passes ordinary96.564 and prose55.977, with
  matching whole-quality artifact/output identities. Both successes are warm,
  short infilling with declared preprocessing/loading exclusions, not chatbot
  conversion or general n-scaling evidence. The catalog's initial M364 success
  wording was corrected after primary inspection.
- METH506/507/508/509: source-equivalent sparse arithmetic retains quantized
  source discrepancies. Expert IDs are neither necessary nor sufficient for
  differing generated tokens; selected normalized masses change too, and the
  initial state discrepancy occurs before the first sparse router. Full source
  head on candidate states recovers20 changes, introduces3, leaves14; top8
  refinement reproduces that finite source-head oracle, not all donor decisions.
  A route interface must preserve IDs AND masses, and head repair cannot be
  assumed to correct state-history drift. M506's original admission/custody and
  economic failures remain explicit despite its exact native wires.
- Ternary precision/native and exact integer LUT: full-width source-derived
  quantization can preserve local quality. The tested F64 LUT and exact24-path
  digit/trit LUT are nevertheless4.2--4.6x and8.5--9.1x slower than their dense/
  integer controls. Compressed resident payload alone is insufficient: count
  repeated plane reads, decoding, table construction and actual selected work.
- Conditional output banks: tested affine images already fail even optimistic
  all-expert target-aware coverage. A better router for those unchanged banks
  cannot establish the missing output geometry. Do not repeat leaf-count/rank
  grids without a different representation or changed source-derived mechanism.

## Consequences for the next experiment

The [physical-worker screen](PHYSICAL_WORKER_ORGANS_RESULT_20261014.md) is fully
closed and all supplied-operand outputs exact, yet generator/readout partial
groups remain~45ms with three workers. More workers alone do not reach20ms.
Do not resume the unexecuted6/12 depth grid solely to chase a cheap fixed core,
or combine Qwen FFN timing with Switch head timing and Falcon recurrence quality.

A next candidate must explicitly reduce active coefficient traffic/arithmetic
while keeping or adapting useful source functions. First cost and qualify the
actual encoded generators/readout on known histories, including decode/routing
overhead, then compose and judge own-history chatbot behavior on the same pack.
INT8/INT4 generator encoding and a lossless narrow-byte integer readout are
prospective mechanisms, not existing speed or quality passes. The latter can
exploit the admitted bounded p6 integer coefficient range without extra model
approximation; its native decode/correction cost still needs measurement.

Useful n requires distinct source-derived functions, a charged input-driven
route emitting masses as well as IDs, controlled removal/permutation evidence,
matched active work and fresh whole-quality evaluation. Physical DRAM,
multi-family/10B/100B applicability and sustainable conversion costs remain open.
No new numerical run is launched by this readback.
