# METH-180: direct pretrained GigaChat expert rank screen

**Uncertainty and prior evidence.** The pinned real GigaChat 3.1 Lightning 10B-A1.8B source has 64 pretrained routed experts per MoE layer and a source-bound BF16 base GGUF. Its quality-valid Q4 base addresses about 1,016 MB/token, including 356 MB of routed experts, above the 560 MB/token design allotment at 40 GB/s. A byte-competitive low-rank representation might reduce expert traffic while preserving the useful pretrained experts, but no spectrum of these actual weights has been measured. METH-06/08 show that direct low-bit expert quantization harms model quality. This screen asks whether *best-case unweighted* low-rank reconstruction at a rank that could halve Q4 expert bytes has even plausible weight fidelity; it does not score activations or the model.

**Frozen sample and math.** Read only base layers 1, 13 and 25, expert IDs 0, 32 and 63, and `gate_proj`, `up_proj`, `down_proj` from the source snapshot at revision `189fff27a1dee68473960c3d5bca53e0e07a3191`: 27 BF16 tensors with shapes 1280×1536 or 1536×1280. Exclude layer 26 MTP. Bind the prior source-binding report SHA-256 `0cd0699fb100e66eb89b37ed088a942228446c5b20939dcc682eec186772182f`, source index and exact shard hashes. Convert BF16 to FP32, compute full singular values with a six-thread local LAPACK, verify their squared sum against the matrix Frobenius norm, and report the optimal rank-64/128/192/384/512/768 Frobenius energy fractions and residual RMS for every tensor, plus pooled min/median/max by projection and layer. Record every input tensor's BF16 SHA-256. Rank 192 has `192×(1280+1536)=540,672` factor elements versus 1,966,080 original elements: at one byte/factor it is 27.50% of BF16 payload and 50% of an ideal 0.55-byte/element Q4 payload before scales, metadata or kernel overhead. The exact GGUF routed-expert bytes stay the cost reference, not a fabricated uniform Q4 artifact.

**Decision.** If median rank-192 energy across the 27 tensors is below 95% or any sampled tensor is below 90%, do not promote a direct, weight-only rank-192 int8 factor export as the next full-model candidate. Investigate activation-aware, outlier-preserving or trained factorization instead. Passing this optimistic screen would only permit a full-model quality/cost experiment with exact export and CPU factor-kernel timing. Neither outcome proves that the entire GigaChat source fits 50 tok/s or that its expert count scales to 100B.

**Budget.** Local CPU only, no T4 or GPU. Stop after 15 minutes, above 6 GiB process RSS, or on source hash/shape/numeric mismatch. Do not write transformed model weights; save only small JSON results. Preserve partial rows and the failed stage if interrupted.
