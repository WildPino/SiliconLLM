# Repository evidence for the pipeline reassessment (15 October 2026)

Scope: read-only audit of retained reports and implementation. The live distinct-source capture was not touched. This note distinguishes measured stage costs, composed partial costs, semantic quality, and untested pipeline hypotheses. The target remains same-artifact donor-relative chatbot quality and at least 50 accepted batch-one tokens/s with a compact reusable core and useful conditional capacity; see `../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/INDEX.md:8-15`.

## Claim checks

1. **“Contextual INT4 generators take 13.8 ms on three physical cores” is not supported by the retained generator reports.** The source uses 23 contextual `in_proj` matrices, 2,048 input coordinates and 315,785,216 distinct coefficients (`CONTEXTUAL_GENERATOR_COST_RESULT_20261014.md:6-12,53-59`). In the three-worker codec assay, I4 group-128 **generators alone** take 22.80625 ms resident / 23.84020 ms evicted median; 13.8 ms is not that assay's median (`CONTEXTUAL_GENERATOR_CODEC_RESULT_20261014.md:7-13,28-33`). The interleaved generator plus **24 SSM readouts** takes 24.12520 / 22.44160 ms in the active I4 arm, with four of six passive drift checks failing and a retained 1,211.725 s interruption (`ENCODED_ORGANS_WAIT_RESULT_20261014.md:8-28`). Its C implementation includes the codec source in `benchmarks/native_expert_scaling/encoded_organs_wait.c:2`; the experiment is not a joined decoder. The I4 source-field worst relative error is 15.283569%, against a historical 1% screen, on supplied original own-history inputs; no evolving model quality was evaluated (`CONTEXTUAL_GENERATOR_CODEC_RESULT_20261014.md:21-26`). Even the BF 3-worker interleaved baseline is 45.02860 / 45.47035 ms, with generator components 28.94605 / 29.21445 ms; these medians are not additive (`PHYSICAL_WORKER_ORGANS_RESULT_20261014.md:25-41`). **Inference:** a 13.8 ms memory or arithmetic lower bound cannot be drawn from these results; nor can 13.8 ms be used as a validated 23-generator cost. Next falsifier for a new codec design: prespecify source-field and own-history quality gates, then time the exact joined 23-generator/24-readout artifact with all output cells audited; the partial median must leave a credible budget for recurrence, FFN, attention, LM head, routing and frontend.

2. **The 10–12 ms “readout” is an SSM output projection, not a 65k-vocabulary LM head.** The qualified integer readout maps normalized 3,072-dimensional SSM output to 2,048 dimensions, with 2,048×3,072 coefficients (`INTEGER_READOUT_RESULT_20261013.md:11-24`). The activation-LUT study's 12.27115/12.55825 ms readout-only medians are for 24 such projections after recurrent blocks; the same report warns that these are not subtractable stage costs and excludes the LM head (`NATIVE_BF_ACTIVATION_LUT_RESULT_20261014.md:25-29,52-57`). The source token ID range to 65,536 is an input-table range in the standalone native SSM branch; that branch explicitly lacks the LM head and tokenizer (`TOKEN_STATE_RESULT_20261013.md:10-33,78-104`). Replacing its 24 internal readouts with a vocabulary shortlist confuses incompatible dimensions and functions. Next falsifier: instrument one joined native block/decoder with labeled SSM projection and final vocabulary-head clocks and verify dimensions and logits independently before attributing any time savings.

3. **METH510's sub-1 ms hybrid head is a real but narrow Switch result.** It runs an old I8 full vocabulary pass, selects top eight, recomputes only those logits using already-present F32 tied embedding rows, and retains all other I8 logits (`METH_510_NATIVE_HEAD_RESULT_20261006.md:8-20`). The vocabulary head is 32,128 rows × 768 coordinates: 98,697,216 F32 source bytes and 24,674,304 I8 code bytes per position plus scales (`METH_509_SHORTLIST_HEAD_RESULT_20261006.md:40-47`; `METH_510_NATIVE_HEAD_RESULT_20261006.md:70-74`). On 996 fixed states and three physical cores, head-only original mean 0.847083 ms, hybrid 0.995200 ms, **0.148116 ms slower**, with p95 increment 0.619665 ms and a retained 30.5045 ms maximum (`METH_510_NATIVE_HEAD_RESULT_20261006.md:23-29,43-74`). In METH511 the same Switch conditional-WO artifact passes declared fresh infilling quality: all-position top-one agreement 0.98735119 and warm prose 52.55861 accepted IDs/s (lower 95% 50.46847), but six of 24 books regress on the fixed cost criterion, so the recipe is ineligible (`METH_511_WHOLE_HYBRID_HEAD_RESULT_20261007.md:39-64,66-89,128-139`). This does not establish 98.7% general chatbot quality, a 65k Falcon head, cold performance or a sub-1 ms replacement for 24 SSM readouts. The implementation is present behind `SILICON_SWITCH_HYBRID_HEAD` in `benchmarks/phase60/engine.c:5`, but its 506/511 Switch payload and full-WI/128-parent route remain a separate architecture (`METH_511_WHOLE_HYBRID_HEAD_RESULT_20261007.md:11-24,130-139`). Next falsifier for transfer: adapt the shortlist idea to the actual Falcon final head dimensions and candidate distribution, predeclare ID/mass, NLL/top-one, long-tail and own-generation gates, and measure it joined after the unchanged full decoder; do not substitute METH510 timing.

4. **Sparse 24-layer FFN/MoE is an opportunity, not an integrated result.** The existing full-width bridge retains source GPU FFN/attention/head; its 14/16 known tasks and DEV KL 0.02156 are a quality control, not a CPU native chatbot (`INDEX.md:50-62`). All 24 FFN operands are captured, but the exact per-token remainder selector evaluates *all* 4,608 source neurons before choosing a sparse subset. Local mean/worst gates pass at tested counts 512/2048/32 for sites 0/12/23; this is explicitly an expensive oracle and only three sites (`FFN_REMAINDER_ORACLE_RESULT_20261013.md:6-20,27-60`). A deployable source-linear PCA sketch selector was implemented in Python (`benchmarks/native_expert_scaling/ffn_active_router.py:18-28`), but its evaluator still uses full source operators after selection and no physical reduced-cost kernel exists (`FFN_SKETCH_ROUTER_RESULT_20261014.md:6-19`). No tested rank passes FIT at sites 0/12; site 23 FIT rank64 fails DEV, so no candidate is admitted (`FFN_SKETCH_ROUTER_RESULT_20261014.md:24-50`). The affine 1/16/64-cell variants likewise fail FIT (`FFN_AFFINE_DEFECT_RESULT_20261013.md:15-35`). Unadapted 3/6/12 kept-block depth schedules fail FIT (12 blocks KL 5.447008/disagreement 81.87035%); the six-block diagnostic also fails DEV/tasks (`FIXED_DEPTH_FIT_RESULT_20261015.md:6-18`; `FIXED_DEPTH_DIAGNOSTIC_RESULT_20261015.md:15-19`). Balanced 512-step joint replay improves repeated FIT (KL 0.70077), but DEV KL 7.11348/disagreement 81.48655% fails and has no native/rate admission (`CHATBOT_BALANCED_REPLAY_RESULT_20261015.md:8-20,32-37`). These failures rule out the **tested** selectors, static omission, and current training coverage; they do not prove sparse conditional FFNs or learned block merging impossible. Next falsifier: after the ongoing distinct-source capture passes its separate adoption audit, freeze a source-informed conditional selector/FFN adaptation with a held-out quality gate; require selected IDs **and normalized masses**, compiled selected-row/DRAM cost at the middle-layer 2,048-active case, then joined 24-layer own-history quality and same-artifact rate. Stop before a full-bank fit if the local selector or physical cost gate fails.

## Highest-value sequencing

The strongest reusable pieces today are the exact BF activation LUT (24 recurrent blocks 16.81725/17.02385 ms median, leaving only 3.18275/2.97615 ms of a 20 ms target; `NATIVE_BF_ACTIVATION_LUT_RESULT_20261014.md:6-29,86-104`), its verified loader `benchmarks/native_expert_scaling/bf_activation_backend.py:17`, and the exact integer SSM readout (`INTEGER_READOUT_RESULT_20261013.md:11-28`). They are available but not joined into the accepted chatbot. A major reduction must target active work and/or executed depth across contextual generators and FFN, with adaptation to recover behavior. Repricing another minor readout layout cannot bridge the measured I4 partial cost. Keep the current source capture untouched; its additional FIT/DEV coverage addresses the demonstrated generalization gap, but source packets alone do not prove any fast target architecture (`CHATBOT_BALANCED_REPLAY_RESULT_20261015.md:58-63`; `INDEX.md:19-40`).
