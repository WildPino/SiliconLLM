# Architecture and conversion literature: decision memo

15 October 2026. Scope: primary papers, official model materials, and the repository's measured constraints. This is a source and feasibility review, not a new model result. The operational objective remains a reproducible path from pretrained chat model to the C engine, with useful distinct conditional capacity, route IDs **and masses**, donor-relative quality, and at least 50 accepted batch-1 tokens/s on the **same** artifact across more than one family.

## Recommendation

Keep two competing transfer tracks alive, with an early byte and quality gate. **Track A** starts from an already recurrent, routed pretrained *instruct* model, IBM Granite-4.0-H-Tiny, and preserves its learned router and distinct experts while changing the expensive precision/representation. It is the cleanest test of whether the engine can retain *pretrained conditional capacity* without first inventing it. **Track B** starts from a dense pretrained model and replaces its attention mixer by weight-initialized SSM/hybrid blocks, then tests a separately justified FFN-to-conditional-memory conversion. The literature supports mixer transfer; it does **not** provide a cheap complete conversion of FFNs, head, depth, and width into this target. Track B therefore remains a transfer-method research route, not the next deployable artifact. Reject unchanged 24-block Falcon full-width completion and direct Granite Q4 port as 50-token candidates under the current CPU budget.

The reason is concrete. The measured Falcon branch with 23 encoded generators and 24 readouts already takes 24.125 ms resident / 22.442 ms evicted at I4-g128 **before** conv/state/norm, FFN, attention, head, router and frontend; its stability gate also failed [local encoded-organ result](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/ENCODED_ORGANS_WAIT_RESULT_20261014.md). The full-width native chatbot is a quality bridge (14/16 tasks, DEV KL .02156) that retains source GPU FFN/attention/head, not a complete CPU converter [local native-chat result](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/FULL_WIDTH_NATIVE_CHAT_RESULT_20261013.md). The compact D512/L12/top8/E72 replay's new FIT KL .701 became new DEV KL 7.113 and 81.49% disagreement after 512 updates, so “a small student can learn it” is not supported by this run [local replay](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/CHATBOT_BALANCED_REPLAY_RESULT_20261015.md). Its failure does not prove a compact core cannot work after changed data, initialization, or conditional mechanism.

## What the papers actually transfer

| [Drop-Upcycling](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.19261), §§3,5 | Partially reinitializes copied experts to encourage specialization. | [MoEfication](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.01786), §3 | Groups coactivated neurons and trains a selector for sparse use of an existing FFN. | Its demonstrated sparse construction depends on activation structure and selector quality. That is a local rejection of the tested selector, not of all structured FFN conversion. |

The combined lesson is asymmetrical: retaining pretrained *width/depth/FFN* makes cross-architecture distillation tractable, while making the active core compact and the FFN conditional is a second, harder operation. Published mixer transfers must be judged on their active bytes after their retained organs are counted. The proposed compact core with frozen large memory has the better theoretical latency shape, but the local D512 replay shows its knowledge transfer is currently unqualified. Preserving 24 source blocks has a better local quality bridge but a measured latency floor above target even before several organs are added.

## Native routed donor and exact semantics

The [official Granite-4.0-H-Tiny instruct card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-4.0-h-tiny), “Model Summary,” “Model Architecture,” and benchmark table, identifies an Apache-2.0 7B total/~1B active **instruct** checkpoint derived from the base model, with 40 layers (36 Mamba2, 4 attention), width 1536, 64 routed experts and top-6, a 1024-wide shared expert, 512-wide routed experts, and tied input/output embeddings. Its card reports chat/instruction evaluations; these are IBM's reported results, not a local quality baseline. The inspected official [config.json](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-4.0-h-tiny/blob/main/config.json) specifies vocab 100352, state 128, Mamba expand 2, no position embedding in the attention path, residual multiplier .22, embedding multiplier 12, logits scaling 6, and tied embeddings. The [reference implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/main/src/transformers/models/granitemoehybrid/modeling_granitemoehybrid.py), `GraniteMoeHybridTopKRouter` and `GraniteMoeHybridExperts`, computes F32 router logits, selects top-k logits, softmaxes **within those selected logits**, and multiplies each selected expert output by its weight. Thus a C converter must preserve ID tie order, mass normalization, shared-path addition, gate/up SiLU/down layout, scaling, recurrent state and four attention positions. “Top-6 IDs” alone is a wrong model.

Local work already screened the **base**, whose geometry is the relevant starting bound: [METH-02](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/METH_02_GRANITE_H_TINY_METADATA_SCREEN_20260925.md) counted 6.939B total and 1.465B active large weights/token; [METH-03](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/METH_03_GRANITE_Q4_ACTIVE_LEDGER_20260925.md) inspected the official base GGUF tensor header (not full values), charging 913.314 MB of active Q4_K_M payload/token: routed 342.835, SSM 298.969, tied head 126.444, shared FFN 114.278, router 15.729, attention 14.561 MB. At the repository's 40 GB/s CPU yardstick that is 22.833 ms **ideal read time alone**, requiring 353.314 MB (38.68%) reduction to meet its 14 ms streaming allotment. The base ledger is a geometry bound, not a measured instruct rate. [METH-412](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/METH_412_GRANITE_I8_COST_RESULT_20261004.md) and METH-413 rejected a specific **Granite 3.1** synthetic row-I8 CPU kernel cost path before value acquisition. They do not reject Granite-4.0-H-Tiny quality or a transformed packed format.

The subsequent [instruct header preflight](NATIVE_DONOR_PREFLIGHT.md) completed that narrower metadata gate for safetensors: 586 tensors, 1.46547B addressed coefficients/token and 779.764 MB/token under an explicitly hypothetical all-I4/group128-scaled matrix format. It excludes source-value and chat-template acquisition. Its result further rules out unchanged I4 as a credible 14 ms streaming path, while leaving a transformed-format and quality experiment open.

[Jamba](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.19887), abstract and Table 1, is a larger native hybrid-MoE control: 52B total/12B active with attention and Mamba and a 4 GB KV cache at 256K. It proves the design family can host large conditional capacity, not suitability to 12 GB or this CPU budget. [Zamba](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16712), abstract, uses a recurrent backbone and one shared attention module but is dense; its 7B model was pretrained on 1T tokens. It informs compact repeated mixer design, not donor-derived conditional memory. Neither is a substitute for proving retained *distinct* experts in the produced artifact.

## Precision and physical budget

[Quamba](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13229), §3, finds the recurrent SSM input and output have different outlier sensitivity from attention and uses tailored 8-bit treatment. [Quamba2](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.22879), §6 and Tables 8–10, supports W4A16/W4A8 Mamba-family post-training quantization, but its 8B LAMBADA ablation moves W4A16 from 64.7% simple per-group to 71.2% after Hadamard and GPTQ; the FP16 value is 71.2%. On W4A8 blocks, adding a 4-bit head barely changes its 8B LAMBADA 68.5% to 68.3%, but adding both head and embedding has model-dependent effects. This is **not** a Granite/ Falcon head guarantee. [Ternary Mamba](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18114), abstract, §3.2 and §8, grouped QAT of pretrained Mamba2-1.3B uses 102M tokens / 4 H100 GPU-hours for 3.61× **storage** compression, but leaves embedding, LM head, SSM dynamics, conv and norms FP16. Its zero-shot average drops from FP16 55.5 to 48.1; packed 2-bit inference kernel is not provided. The authors explicitly limit evidence to one Mamba2-1.3B English model. It motivates selective ternary projections with protected sensitive organs; it does not validate ternary whole-model quality or CPU acceleration.

For this C engine the **CPU** 40 GB/s yardstick implies at most 800 MB of *ideal one-pass* weight traffic in 20 ms, before compute, state, routing, sampling and OS noise; 560 MB/14 ms is the declared streaming sub-budget. The [RTX 3060 official specification](https://www.nvidia.com/en-gb/geforce/news/game-on-you-asked-we-answered-qa/) gives 12 GB and 360 GB/s *GPU* memory bandwidth, irrelevant as a substitute for CPU DRAM bandwidth or unmeasured PCIe streaming. It does bound local training: a dense 1.3B Adam update with FP32 weight, gradient and two moments alone needs about 20.8 GB decimal before activations, optimizer overhead or teacher, so full-parameter fine-tuning does not fit 12 GB without offload/sharding/other optimizer representation. Eight A100×80GB for days (Mamba in the Llama) and one H100×4h (Ternary Mamba) must be treated as published resources, not converted into an unsupported 3060 ETA. A 7B teacher plus full trainable student on 3060 would require a separately measured offload/LoRA/distillation budget. The existing D512/L12 replay spent ~2 h GPU for 512 updates and still failed new DEV [local replay](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/CHATBOT_BALANCED_REPLAY_RESULT_20261015.md); more steps on the same small cohort are not a resource or quality solution.

The Falcon full head alone is 65537×2048 = 134,219,776 coefficients: 268.44 MB at I16, 134.22 MB at I8, 67.11 MB at ideal I4, before scales and computation. METH510's 0.9952 ms hybrid head result is a **Switch** V32128/H768 component measurement, not a Falcon head promise [METH510](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/METH_510_NATIVE_HEAD_RESULT_20261006.md). Any reuse of its timing across these shapes is invalid. A learned routing table growing to 10B/100B also costs storage and potentially scoring/metadata bandwidth even with fixed top-k; an O(E·d) flat router can erase active-expert savings. Separate the number of *distinct* parameters, active coefficients, bytes actually read, and donor capability retained.

## Three decision-changing tests, in order

1. **Native donor metadata and byte/semantic gate (CPU only): completed for config/index/headers.** [The instruct preflight](NATIVE_DONOR_PREFLIGHT.md) pinned a revision and counted all 586 tensors without weights. All-I4 misses the 560 MB streaming sub-budget by 219.764 MB. A prospective mixed/structural format must get below that bound with explicit head/router/SSM allowances and compute margin before source-value acquisition. Tokenizer/chat-template semantics are a later model-execution prerequisite. A new measured format could reverse the current rejection of direct Q4; a card benchmark or synthetic expert count cannot.
2. **One complete transformed Granite organ path.** On pinned real values and held-out chat cases, preserve exact top-6 IDs/masses, shared path and tied head; substitute/quantize only the expensive SSM and FFN projections under an explicit byte cap. Measure local donor-relative KL, disagreement, task behavior and active bytes in C. Stop on failed frozen quality or full cost gate. A passing *same-artifact* complete batch-1 ≥50 result would make the native-MoE path primary; local organ fidelity alone would not.
3. **Controlled dense-donor transfer alternative.** On one tractable transformer donor, test weight-initialized mixer replacement with MOHAWK-style matrix/block alignment and held-out whole-model distillation, then a **separate** conditional FFN path using actual source-derived expert diversity and cheap input-conditioned IDs+masses. Freeze source/target shapes, trainable parameters, tokens/GPU-hour budget, donor-relative held-out and accepted C cost criteria before training. Stop if projected active bytes already exceed budget or aligned mixer quality collapses. This route becomes primary only if it retains distinct donor capacity and passes full-artifact cost/quality, then repeats on a second family.

The evidence that would overturn the recommendation is a measured, stable 24-block CPU artifact under 20 ms with all organs and donor-relative quality, or a compact-core result with held-out chat quality and demonstrably useful large conditional memory. Neither exists. The current source-only donor capture is live; none of these tests requires disturbing it or claiming an outcome from it.

## Source and inference boundaries

Primary sources were inspected at their linked abstract, method, tables, configuration or reference implementation on 15 October 2026. Numbers from publications are source-reported, not independently reproduced here. Local linked results are measurements or audited artifact arithmetic with their own limitations. “Ideal read time” is a division of addressed bytes by an assumed bandwidth; it is a lower-bound screen under a one-pass streaming model, not PMU DRAM traffic or an execution measurement. The proposed transfer stages and decision tests are hypotheses. No literature source demonstrates a general 10B–100B pretrained-to-this-engine conversion, a 3060-scale full-parameter conversion of the 7B Granite instruct model, or useful ≥50 accepted C tokens/s from these candidates.
