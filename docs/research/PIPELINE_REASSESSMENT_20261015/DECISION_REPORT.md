# Pipeline reassessment: preserve knowledge before reducing active work

15 October 2026. Research decision; no new model is admitted. The original goal remains incomplete.

**Later owner scope clarification:** [native MoE family scaling is sufficient](../NATIVE_EXPERT_SCALING_20260925/SCOPE_CLARIFICATION_20261015.md).
Dense70B conversion is not mandatory; one family including very large models
is sufficient. References below to obligatory unrelated-family tests or a
required dense-transfer alternative describe the earlier interpretation.
Quality, useful retained capacity and same-artifact CPU rate remain mandatory.

## Executive Summary

The current sequence needs a strategic change. The compact target learns repeated FIT examples but still fails unseen DEV, while the unchanged full-width path exceeds the latency budget before several model organs are counted. Continuing the same fit or refining a small exact kernel does not address both failures. This conclusion follows from the closed local experiments, rather than from an assumption that either compact students or sparse models are impossible. [1]

The proposed procedure starts from the pretrained function and reduces active work under a complete byte and quality budget. It has two family-dependent entry points: preserve the learned router and distinct experts of a native recurrent MoE chatbot; or partition a dense donor FFN exactly before learning sparse selection and contribution masses. In both cases, preserve sensitive recurrent semantics and calibrate expensive projections for an executable packed format. Published work supports these ingredients separately; it does not demonstrate their complete composition in this engine. [2] [3] [4] [5]

The implemented metadata-only instruct-donor preflight validates all 586 tensor headers at a pinned revision. It exposes per-organ bytes and protected precision without acquiring tensor values. Its all-I4 scenario still costs 779.764 MB/token. A mixed 2/3/4-bit scenario reduces that to 554.586 MB, but has neither quality nor a compiled kernel; state/cache traffic further consumes the margin. The previously screened Granite base geometry already rejects an unchanged Q4 path; changing checkpoint names does not reverse that finding. [1] [6]

Keep the ongoing source capture as reusable supervision, but do not automatically launch another D512 replay when it finishes. The next substantive GPU experiment must change the transfer mechanism or the operator representation and pass the new budget gate first. A passing component, a large expert count, or a source-reported benchmark is insufficient: the final claim still requires preserved chatbot quality, useful conditional capacity, physical memory accounting and at least 50 accepted batch-one CPU tokens/s on one artifact. [1]

## Introduction

The question is a procedure, not a preferred checkpoint. The authoritative goal requires pretrained chatbot knowledge transferred to a reusable C core with useful selectively consulted capacity. It allows different transformations for different donor families, but requires the same produced artifact to pass quality and rate. A donor-specific first success is useful; generality requires a second family or scale and explicit prerequisites. The review therefore considers both native conditional donors and dense donors, rather than silently replacing the goal with a generic runtime or a small distilled chatbot. [1]

This report combines an audit of retained local measurements, primary literature and official model materials. The three companion reviews give detailed locators: [repository audit](REPOSITORY_EVIDENCE.md), [sparsity review](SPARSITY_LITERATURE.md), [architecture review](ARCHITECTURE_LITERATURE.md). The [calibration feasibility map](CALIBRATION_FEASIBILITY.md) identifies real operands and missing captures. Source-reported results are not reproduced here. Proposed transforms, thresholds and sequencing are labeled as decisions or hypotheses. No GPU learner, rate benchmark or weight acquisition was started during this review.

## Main Analysis

### Finding 1: the problem is joint transfer and active cost

The latest balanced replay completed 512 additional updates and passed its stored-output audit. New FIT KL was 0.70077, whereas new DEV KL was 7.11348 with 81.487% disagreement; old DEV also failed. This is a material generalization failure for that target, initialization, data and training recipe. It is not evidence that more parameter-free bookkeeping can solve knowledge transfer. It also does not identify a universal representation limit: the experiment does not separate insufficient coverage, the projection bottleneck, conditional-bank design and optimization. The defensible action is to stop unchanged replay and change a variable that addresses one of those mechanisms. [1]

The opposite route, keeping the original full-width functions, has a valuable quality control but an unresolved cost problem. The native recurrent bridge preserves useful behavior on its known panel while source FFN, attention and vocabulary head remain on GPU. It proves that those substituted recurrent functions can compose into a useful chatbot; it does not prove a compact CPU model. The I4 generator plus integer readout experiment measures only a supplied-operand partial path and is already above 20 ms. It also retains failed stability controls. These are implementation-specific observations, not a theorem against every low-bit kernel, but they reject treating the remaining model organs as free. [1]

Two numerical conflations obscure that conclusion. The approximately 12 ms internal readout comprises 24 SSM projections from 3,072 channels to the 2,048-dimensional residual stream. The final vocabulary projection is a separate 65,537-by-2,048 matrix. A vocabulary shortlist cannot replace the former. METH510's 0.9952 ms hybrid head belongs to Switch with vocabulary 32,128 and width 768; it refines an existing I8 screen and is slower than that I8 screen alone. Its 98.7% figure belongs to a subsequent bounded whole-model agreement result, not a universal guarantee for another head. The retained reports also do not support 13.8 ms as the validated 23-generator I4 median. [1]

These corrections change the experiment order, not the usefulness of the ideas. A fast shortlist remains worth testing on the actual final head. Sparse FFNs remain necessary candidates for reducing large selected coefficient streams. Recurrent projections remain candidates for calibrated packing and hardware-compatible lookup. The mistake would be adding favorable timings from different artifacts and declaring that sum a complete decoder. The new control must join all these organs under one representation, one runtime configuration and a quality-qualified own-history execution. Until then, each component result has its stated scope.

The desired first milestone is consequently a credible complete cost envelope with an initially faithful function. The envelope must charge dense projections, router IDs and masses, selected expert payloads, recurrent state, attention and cache traffic at declared contexts, vocabulary scoring, sampling and any rejected work. It must separately record total stored parameters and conversion state. This deliberately changes the decision from “which isolated experiment passes?” to “which transformation can leave enough budget for every mandatory organ?” It still allows a stage to be studied independently, provided its result changes that complete decision.

An early local gate saves training only if it is relevant to the final claim. A projection-relative-error threshold is a useful calibration diagnostic, but is not interchangeable with chatbot quality. Similarly, a memory division is a screening calculation, not a CPU timing. The report preserves both kinds of gate and their limitations. If a changed representation fails a local screen, retain the failure; if whole-model evaluation is prescribed, complete it without inventing a quality conclusion from the local proxy. No threshold is relaxed after examining DEV. [1]

### Finding 2: preserve the donor function before sparsifying it

The useful commonality in sparse conversion papers is pretrained initialization, not a claim that a few routed rows automatically recover a large model. Granular upcycling explicitly addresses the scaling error introduced when donor FFNs are split and weighted. MoEfication shows a route from partitions of an existing FFN to selective execution, but under particular activations, tasks and training conditions. Dense-to-recurrent transfer work likewise retains substantial donor structure rather than simultaneously discarding width, depth and all nonlinear functions. These are separate demonstrations with different limitations. [2] [3] [7] [8]

For a bias-free SwiGLU FFN, partition the intermediate coordinates into disjoint sets. If `f(x) = W_down [SiLU(W_gate x) * (W_up x)]`, define each shard by the corresponding gate/up rows and down columns. Then `f(x) = sum_j f_j(x)` in real arithmetic. This is an algebraic identity, not an empirical sparsity claim. Floating-point reduction order can still change results and must be qualified against the actual source convention. Bias, tensor orientation, fused layouts, scaling and quantization boundaries belong to the importer contract. Source values must be stored once rather than counted repeatedly as new knowledge.

The identity gives a better starting control than the current narrow projected student: every original nonlinear contribution is available initially. All shards selected with contribution mass one reconstruct the original function. A sum-to-one router cannot simply be inserted at that point: it attenuates the residual unless the expert output or router scale compensates exactly. This is a concrete reason to implement IDs and masses together. For a native MoE donor the contract is different: preserve its learned selected-logit normalization, shared experts and output scaling, rather than replacing its masses with the dense-partition convention. [2] [6]

The proposed sparse-learning variable is then removal of active contributions under a budget, not learning the entire chatbot again from a few repeated responses. Begin from the all-on partition; freeze the source bank initially; train a small nonlinear router and bounded contribution scales using FIT output and logit losses. Tighten the selected-byte constraint gradually, retaining an exact full-selection control. Compare all-routed and shared-plus-routed layouts at the same actual active-byte allowance. Shared experts are charged on every token. Their role is justified only if their cost buys better held-out fidelity than spending those bytes on conditional shards. [4]

This proposal is deliberately narrower than “upcycle a dense model into many independent experts.” It does not create extra knowledge at initialization. Source shards contain distinct pretrained coordinates, but their selective usefulness remains to be demonstrated. Later added experts must change weights and improve held-out behavior at fixed active work. Route frequency, nonzero weights, a large bank file and parameter count alone cannot establish that gain. Published continued-pretraining budgets for expert diversification are substantial; they are reasons to avoid assuming local low-cost specialization, not reasons to forbid a bounded small-donor trial. [2] [9]

The local hard case is the middle FFN site: an expensive exact oracle needed 2,048 active source neurons in one tested configuration, while cheap tested predictors failed. A new small router must beat those failures with its own inputs and masses; an oracle label or selected-row count is insufficient. Test that bottleneck before a full 24-site adaptation. If only high active fractions preserve the function, the byte planner must reject the candidate unless other organs provide measured savings. If a nonlinear selector works, export it into C and charge its weights, scoring, normalization and gather overhead before claiming speed. [1]

For native routed donors, the more direct experiment retains already learned experts and routing while changing representation. That avoids inventing conditional capacity during the first conversion. It does not bypass the compact-core requirement: a large always-on path can still fail the complete budget. These two entry points should share export contracts, active-byte accounting, numeric checks and quality admission. Their transfer-specific steps should remain different. That is a plausible general procedure with family-dependent prerequisites; it is not a declaration of universal convertibility.

### Finding 3: quantization must target executable operators and sensitive state

The earlier contextual I4 export is a useful baseline rather than a completed quantization method. It uses source-only group scaling and measures decoded-weight arithmetic faithfully; its worst original-source field error is 15.28%. It does not calibrate recurrent behavior or evaluate the resulting complete chatbot on its own history. No retained Falcon artifact jointly calibrates contextual generation and internal output projection. The new variable is therefore an activation-aware, recurrently composed representation, rather than a repetition of the same group-rounding kernel at another thread count. [1]

The relevant literature distinguishes sensitive recurrence inputs, channel outliers and output projection from ordinary transformer linears. Quamba2 combines legal channel/head reordering and tailored state-group handling with calibrated low-bit projections. That supports testing separate precision policies and output-space transforms; it does not authorize an arbitrary rotation inside a nonlinear or recurrent operator. A permutation must carry convolution, state/head assignments, normalization, skip terms and projection coordinates consistently. A transform at the output projection must include its actual online cost and changed rounding. The project's native state-96 approximation also requires its own controls; a result on the original Mamba state does not certify it. [5]

An executable low-bit format matters as much as the count of packed coefficients. T-MAC uses activation-dependent tables and SIMD register lookups over bit planes. Its official repository cautions that older x86 systems, especially at four bits, may not realize large gains. AQLM offers another codebook design, but its published CPU comparison and conversion budget do not establish cheap local adaptation. These are alternative implementation hypotheses, not performance numbers to substitute for the existing Ryzen measurements. The previously failed exact integer byte/trit lookup remains failed; a changed approximate format must explicitly identify its different arithmetic, lookup construction and access pattern. [10] [11] [12]

For the dense Falcon route, the first representation test can reuse real FIT contextual inputs, source BF matrices and integer readout packets. The feasibility map identifies a crucial missing operand: output-projection inputs on the changed recurrence trajectory. Original-source supplied operands cannot answer that question. A sequential CPU shadow must regenerate the changed state before calibrating or judging the next organ. Full 24-site original-history readout inputs are also less complete than the three-site captures. Capture cost and replay dependencies must be included in the protocol, rather than silently treating all required operands as already available. [1]

The test must distinguish three things. First, the unmodified full-width bridge is the numerical and useful-quality control. Second, source-only I4 rounding is the baseline whose limitations are already known. Third, the new activation-aware G/output representation is the hypothesis. Keep source depth, FFN and attention unchanged while comparing those representations; only then add learned FFN sparsity. Otherwise a failure mixes recurrence quantization, dropped nonlinear capacity and a new router, leaving no useful diagnosis. This is a controlled path toward a complete transformation, not a claim that full-width source execution is the deployment result.

For the native MoE route the same discipline starts with complete tensor metadata and the actual instruction checkpoint. The previous Granite base Q4 header ledger charges 913.314 MB of active packed payload per token. At an assumed 40 GB/s this alone takes 22.833 ms, before compute. That is an analytical one-pass screen, not measured physical traffic. A direct instruct port with unchanged formats has no demonstrated solution to that arithmetic. The preflight must identify which SSM, head, shared and routed projections change format, the required reduction, their protected precision and the quality risks. Merely pointing at seven billion stored parameters and one billion advertised active parameters misses this decision. [1] [6]

This also constrains optimizer choice. Keep source banks frozen where possible; optimize a bounded router, scales, codebooks or small adapters rather than assuming full dense Adam state is affordable. Report parameter masters, gradients, moments, cached teacher packets and checkpoint writes separately. A source-reported accelerator training run cannot be converted into a local duration by scaling advertised FLOPS. If calibration requires a full-model optimizer or an unavailable capture, it is a different resource proposal and must be priced before launch. Local sustainable conversion is one of the method's requirements, not an accounting appendix added after success. [1] [12]

### Finding 4: the next result should decide a complete pipeline

The recommended immediate branch is a common preflight and two explicit transfer variants. The native-MoE variant is the stronger first candidate for preserving large learned conditional capacity: it begins with distinct experts and a trained router. The dense-donor variant is necessary to investigate broader transfer and provides a tractable local control on exact partitioning and calibrated projections. Neither is selected for deployment in this report. The preflight may reject a donor even if its architecture is conceptually attractive, and a faithful numerical port may still fail the active-cost objective.

The preflight is implemented as a small [metadata tool](../../../benchmarks/native_expert_scaling/conditional_donor_preflight.py) rather than another large run. It pins source revision, config, tensor names, shapes, dtypes and storage extents. Remote requests are bounded header requests; a server returning a complete multi-gigabyte file instead of a requested range is rejected before reading its body. The source index and tensor headers agree completely for the acquired 586-tensor checkpoint. Any unclassified or unpriced tensor is a visible failure, not a zero-cost default. A tied vocabulary matrix is counted once in stored capacity but charged for the scoring operation it performs. The output is an analytical candidate ledger, never measured CPU throughput.

The budget test reports the required streaming bandwidth for the actual selected payload under both the complete 20 ms target and an explicit smaller weight-stream allowance. It includes scales and padding, routers, shared paths and protected tensors. It presents alternative bit widths as hypothetical representations whose quality is unknown. This resolves whether a proposed change is large enough to deserve calibration, without silently equating a code size with a runnable kernel. A format can clear this necessary gate and still fail because of table construction, gathers, recurrent state, attention context or insufficient memory-level parallelism. Native timing remains indispensable.

The next bounded conversion pilot should be frozen only after that screen. For the native donor, require an exact source-routing and operator contract before quantization: IDs, ties, selected masses, shared expert addition, residual/logit scaling, recurrent state and attention positions. For dense Falcon, require all-shard reconstruction and changed-state replay before learning omission. At each stage, preserve a numerical control and log the first defect. No new full-width port is justified merely to rediscover the already excessive active bytes, and no further D512 replay is justified solely because more teacher labels now exist.

Quality selection uses FIT and frozen criteria; DEV evaluates the selected candidate and never chooses a checkpoint or an encoding. The already adopted source corpus can supply more distinct supervision, but does not repair a wrong architecture by definition. Its ongoing donor-only collection should finish and undergo its separate adoption audit. RESERVED remains sealed. Whole-model free-running generation, task behavior and context dependence follow local controls. The method must report both source-prefix fidelity and own-history behavior: a small logit discrepancy can change a generated token, subsequent routes and recurrent history, making teacher-forced agreement an incomplete test. [1]

The final C test must load the same packed weights and router used in quality validation. Measure batch-one accepted output rate, including mandatory per-token work and any rejection or verification overhead, and report startup/prefill separately under declared contexts. Measure or bound actual memory traffic explicitly; addressed coefficient bytes and cache-evicted timing do not magically become a DRAM counter. Demonstrate capacity usefulness with controlled expert ablations or swaps at fixed active budget, not just expert visitation. Grow the bank only after a baseline passes: increasing useful n must leave a measured held-out benefit while router and selected-byte cost remain accounted.

The cross-family test then repeats these admission stages, not necessarily identical transformations. Dense SwiGLU, pretrained MoE, transformer and recurrent donors have different exact identities and state contracts. A ten-billion or hundred-billion checkpoint requires streamed acquisition/conversion and a memory ledger before allocation; optimizer and flat router costs can grow even with fixed top-k. Published large-scale upcycling and mixer distillation establish plausibility at their reported budgets, not the missing local experiment. The procedure is successful only when these staged controls culminate in useful quality and rate on the same artifact. [2] [7] [8] [9]

## Synthesis and Insights

The most useful change is to make knowledge preservation the starting invariant, and the active-byte allowance the constraint that drives transformation. That connects the two original research questions. Exact FFN partitioning provides such an invariant for dense donors; learned native experts provide it for conditional donors; neither automatically supplies a small enough core. Recurrent-sensitive calibration and a suitable packed kernel address core cost, while learned selection addresses conditional-bank cost. Their composition is the hypothesis to test.

A native routed donor is therefore a candidate for the first substantial capacity-preserving result, rather than a model-shopping replacement for the goal. The dense route remains necessary to learn which parts of the procedure transfer beyond native MoE. The cheap preflight prevents a new model family from turning into another full runtime implementation whose active bytes already make the target implausible. It must precede value acquisition, not serve as retrospective documentation.

Speculative verification is a possible later accelerator: the primary algorithm preserves a target distribution using a draft and verification rule. [13] It is deferred here because its acceptance, multi-position recurrent verification, rollback state and union of consulted experts would all need a new complete cost measurement. It also does not by itself establish useful conditional capacity or remove linear scaling of a dense verifier's bytes. A rejected draft token must be charged, and a low-quality student is not made into a successful converted model merely by counting its unverified tokens.

## Limitations and Caveats

No complete converter described here has passed quality and rate. The historical local corpus is finite and partly consumed; original source-prefix and local error results are not fresh held-out behavior. Published methods use other activations, models, hardware and resource budgets. The metadata tool can validate dimensions and cost arithmetic, not source tensor values, learned expert utility or numerical execution. Its alternative bit widths are assumptions until calibrated artifacts and kernels exist.

The 40 GB/s yardstick used in prior Granite screening is an assumption. New required-bandwidth calculations do not certify the local machine can sustain it, nor do they prove a universal lower bound when caching or weight sharing changes physical traffic. The final artifact needs appropriate memory measurements and stable joined timing. A cached coefficient can legitimately reduce traffic, but only if that locality is demonstrated under the declared workload.

## Recommendations

Retain the ongoing 432-case donor-only capture and finish its separate adoption audit. Do not automatically follow it with another unchanged compact replay. Implement and run the pinned instruct-donor metadata preflight; record a complete ledger or an explicit first failure. Compare that ledger with the existing Falcon cost decomposition before authorizing a value download or a full port.

Freeze the next representation pilot only when its proposed active-byte allowance covers the complete model. Prefer retained native routing for the first capacity-preservation track. Keep the dense-source alternative concrete through exact FFN partitioning and the existing changed-state replay hooks. Do not widen the old PCA/grid or static-depth campaign. Treat source-aware G/output calibration, a nonlinear contribution router and a different donor family as distinct variables with separate controls.

For any substantial run, write the uncertainty, reused evidence, decision, cost and stopping rule first. A pilot must specify a fixed FIT-only selector, untouched DEV, whole-model own-history gates, the native export contract and the remaining unmeasured rate terms. If its protected core alone exhausts the budget, revise the architecture before more learner updates. If quality fails, preserve the failure without claiming a universal impossibility or promoting a rate from a degraded artifact.

## Counterevidence Register

The native-MoE recommendation is opposed by the existing Granite active-byte rejection; it is conditional on a different viable representation, not an unchanged Q4 port. The donor-preserving sparse-partition hypothesis is opposed by failed cheap Falcon selectors and a large middle-layer oracle subset. Calibrated recurrent quantization is opposed by the local I4 field error and by published SSM sensitivity. The compact-student alternative is opposed by the new FIT/DEV gap. None of these finite findings establishes universal impossibility, and none can be removed from the decision by citing a favorable paper. [1] [5]

The completed [new instruct preflight](NATIVE_DONOR_PREFLIGHT.md) is additional counterevidence against an easy native-MoE port. Keeping top-6 and source-precision routers, the explicit I2-routed/I3-SSM-and-shared/I4-head-and-attention policy addresses 554,586,240 coefficient bytes/token. Its alternative with I3 routed and I2 SSM addresses 559,562,880. Both almost exhaust the 560 MB weight allowance. A proposed F32 state/ring and F16 full-attention KV implementation additionally addresses about 133.865 MB/cache-update at context 2048. These are logical accounting scenarios, not physical DRAM measurements, source quality or runnable formats. The two policies identify where a major compression hypothesis must succeed; neither is admitted or grounds for downloading the complete model. [1]

## Methodology Appendix

The review checked the original goal, operational index, retained experiment reports and code locators before comparing literature. Independent reviews covered source-claim forensics, sparsity/conversion and architecture/resource feasibility. Primary papers, official model cards and source repositories were preferred over secondary summaries. The local search CLI was unavailable, so retrieval used built-in web search. Evidence records were written before this synthesis and consolidated into stable source, evidence and claim ledgers.

The project requires English repository documentation and compact research entry points; artifacts are therefore kept in this repository rather than creating unrelated Documents copies or presentation exports. No source excerpt is required for a claim where a short paraphrase plus section/table locator suffices. Automated report/citation checks supplement, rather than replace, the source-content readback. No numerical benchmark was run by this review. The source-only job and its completion/adoption status remain separate from this research decision.
