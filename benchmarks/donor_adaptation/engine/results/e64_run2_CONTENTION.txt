E64 run 2 reads its weight artefacts from D:\_ktmp, so the PER-CELL SECONDS logged in
e64_run2.log during this window are CONTENDED and are not timings.  Nothing E64 gates is
affected: every E64 quantity is a BPB, which is deterministic and does not depend on how
long the cell took.  This file exists so that no later reader mistakes those seconds for a
measurement -- "a contended timing is not a timing" cuts both ways, and the cheap half of
the rule is saying so while the contention is still in front of you.
